## 5. The Cordis mapping, and the four handoff requests — TWO TO FILE, TWO MEASURED AWAY

- **THE PREDICATE IS THE RULE AND THE NAME IS AN OPTION** (canonical D-23, research C4, findings 20): no `cordis` package exists in this tree's dependency graph (C3), so what this unit builds is the FOUR PROPERTIES, not a dependency.
