## 2. The shapes — data, and the seam the wire supplies

- **`ChannelMessage = {kind:'write-turn'; turn: number; entries: readonly ChannelEntry[]} | {kind:'registration'; module: string; client: string}`** — the two kinds and no third (L1 §4 `C-15`). **`ChannelEntry = {turn; module; listener; outcome; address?; data?; refusal?}`**, `outcome` the five-arm closed set, `refusal` the two channel-side names only (§5). **`turn` rides the message AND every entry**, because a batch may carry several modules' listeners and a consumer reads an entry without the envelope (L1 §4 `C-2`). **`address?` is the WRITE's address**, carried because the subscription relation compares it with the listener's DECLARED address; **`C-2`'s five-arm shape is re-grained by `ASM-MC-5`** (`C-3`, `M-13`). **`listener` is the declaration's ONE handle** — client-keyed when it names a client half, address-keyed otherwise — and the fan-out fires `fire(listener, entry)` (`C-3`, §7).
- **`StoreRequest = {id: number; verb: ClientStoreVerb; args: readonly unknown[]}`** and **`StoreAnswer = {id: number; answer: unknown}`** — the client act's own envelope, **NOT a `ChannelMessage` kind and NOT the shared `RpcRequest`**: the realm never asks for a source, and `RpcMethod` gains nothing (L1 §4 `C-15`). **`ClientStoreVerb` is the data half of the store surface** (§8).
- **`ChannelTransport` is ONE seam with TWO ends** (the eleventh bridge member, `S-12`): **main** `{push(message); onRequest(handler: (request: StoreRequest) => Promise<StoreAnswer>)}`; **realm** `{onMessage(handler: (message: ChannelMessage) => void): () => void; request(request: StoreRequest): Promise<StoreAnswer>}`. **The wire is free to implement it as one object with two directions; this page adds no second member** (`S-12`, `D-307`).

## 3. The store facade — the binding and the writer's entrance

- **THE CHANNEL IS THE APP'S ONE STORE SURFACE, DECORATED** (`C-2`'s *"wrapped at `central-data`'s adapter seam"*): `createModuleChannel({store, transport}).surface` has the SAME fifteen members as `C-16`'s surface, and every consumer of the app's store holds THIS facade — the executor's `ModuleHost.store`, the data surface, the tab and view records, the operator-settings instance's own construction standing outside it only because it constructs its own store (D-16, `C-16`). **This is why every app write marks a turn**, not only a module's own: a write the app makes to an address a module listens at fires the binding and the writer holds its receipt (`C-2`). **The one-store authority is unmoved**: the facade wraps the ONE instance and constructs no second (D-79, D-80, `C-13`).
- **`listen(handle, [address])` and `register(handle, body)` ARE the binding**: both delegate to the wrapped store, and `register` wraps the body — the body the store holds is the channel's, and it runs the module's main half with the address the store handed it (L1 §3 `S-11`, `C-3`). **A declaration with no `main` half is still registered**; its body records the passthrough address (`D-307`).
- **The body CONTAINS the main half's throw**: it records `{outcome:'threw', refusal:'main-half-threw'}`, returns `undefined`, and the writer's call is unaffected — the store catches nothing and would carry the throw out of an unrelated writer's call (L1 §3 `S-11`, `D-105`). **A returned value is NOT captured here**: the body records only that the handle FIRED, and the writer reads the value off the receipt's `returnedValues` keyed by that handle (`D-310`, `C-3`).
- **The binding record carries the declaration's ONE handle** (`C-3`) — what the store holds and what the realm's `onDelivery` minted — and no second key is carried: the handle is client-keyed when the declaration names a client half, address-keyed otherwise (`ASM-MC-7`).
- **`removeListener` and `unlisten` are the withdrawal's two verbs**, passed through in that order by the executor's inverse; the facade drops the binding's record only after both answer, so a partial teardown leaves the record STANDING and the inverse retryable (L1 §3 `S-23`, `D-105`).
- **The binding RAISES the `reentrant-call` flag around the main half's run, and `module-system`'s ctx READS it** — one value crossing two seats; the ctx's main-half `store` is the refusing member of L2 §10, so the flag makes the refusal the BINDING's ground rather than a stub's, and no store verb or census row moves (L1 §4 `C-2`, `ASM-MC-2`).
- **The other eleven members pass through unchanged** and are the client proxy's data half; **no member is added, renamed or re-typed** (L1 §4 `C-16`, `D-312`).

## 4. The writer and its turn — the receipt is the channel's

- **`write` and `delete` on the facade are the writer's entrance**: the facade calls the wrapped store's own verb, holds the receipt, and marks the turn at the **SETTLED** answer — the receipt when the set is all-synchronous, the `Promise`'s resolution when a started body suspended; **the settle cannot reject** (`D-310`, `H-4`).
- **A turn is EVERY delivering act**: `write`; `delete` on its own member arm; a registration (`addConstraint` and the re-arm); a boot's adopted set; a reversal's legs — each marked at its own return, whichever member carries it (L1 §4 `C-2`, `H-3`). **A read is not a turn**: no receipt, no message.
- **`turn` is a monotone counter the writer holds**, incremented once per delivering act; the writer serialises its own requests, and a main half may not open a verb (§3's flag), so no nested turn can flush before its outer one (L1 §4 `C-2`).
- **The receipt is read BY NAME and never spread**: `returnedValues`, `bodyFailures` and `discarded` are non-enumerable own getters, so a copy loses every body that spoke (`D-310`).
- **The flush is ONE message per turn**, pushed over the transport after the answer; **no entry is pushed piecemeal** (`C-2`, `S-13`).

## 5. The envelope — the five arms, composed from the receipt and the firing record

- **`return`** — the handle is in `returnedValues`; `data` is that SETTLED value; `refusal` absent. **`passthrough`** — the declaration has no main half; the body recorded the address, and `data` is that address. **`empty`** — the body FIRED and the handle is in neither map (the `undefined`-returner's silence); **a rejecting body is this arm's SECOND SHAPE**, its rank-23 `bodyFailures` datum carried beside the entry, the act still answering, and only `write`/`delete` settling (L1 §4 `C-2`, `D-310`). **`threw`** — the body recorded its contained throw; `refusal: 'main-half-threw'`, `data` absent. **`refused`** — the answer cannot cross (a function, a symbol, a cycle, a native handle); `refusal: 'not-transport-safe'`, `data` absent, and **the refusal is the channel's encoder's, after settlement** (`D-310`).
- **A turn may carry NO entry**: a write whose landed literal is the one already standing answers `triggered: []`, no body runs, and `[]` cannot be read as *"no registration stood"* (L1 §4 `C-2`, research A11). **The empty turn is lawful and the realm does nothing.**

## 6. The registration frame — the second kind, and the boot read

- **`registration` carries `{module, client}`**, the module's name and its `client` source STRING, and **answers nothing**: no entry, no receipt, no turn (L1 §4 `C-15`, `D-308`). **The executor produces it** at activation and on the realm's boot read; `createModuleChannel`'s `pushRegistration(frame)` is the one sender `main.ts` wires into `ModuleHost.sendRegistration` (L2 §9).
- **It exists because a boot's registry writes happen before the realm subscribes**: a channel with only write turns would leave a pre-installed module's client half undelivered on every boot (L1 §4 `C-15`). **The realm evaluates the string in `module-system`'s replaced ctx**, registers each listener's client half on `onDelivery`, and the client registry stores `{module, listener} → handler` (§7).

## 7. The realm — one call per module, the prefix relation, containment

- **The realm end receives both kinds**: a `registration` goes to `module-system`'s ctx evaluation (the client source evaluated once per module); a `write-turn` goes to the fan-out.
- **The fan-out groups the entries by `module` and calls EACH MODULE ONCE** with its entries in the message's order — never one call per entry and never one call per listener (L1 §3 `S-13`). **Each entry's client half is looked up by `{module, listener}` and fired ON DELIVERY with the ENTRY**, so the half reads the arm, the address, the data and the refusal it is handed; **`module-system`'s `fire(listener, entry)` is that call — `listener` being the declaration's ONE handle (`ASM-MC-7`) — and its signature is re-grained by `ASM-MC-6`** (L2 §3, `M-11`). A module with no registered client half contributes nothing and is not a fault.
- **The fan-out is ADDRESS-KEYED under the store's prefix relation**, for the crossing-subscriber arm: the entry's address is the WRITE's, and a subscriber is notified when its declared address prefixes that one — **a root subscriber hears a leaf write, a leaf subscriber does not hear a root write** (L1 §4 `C-3`, `M-13`).
- **Containment is PER MODULE**: a client half's throw is caught, answered as that module's datum, and neither its own remaining entries, another module's batch, nor the app's paint is touched (L1 §3 `S-13`). **The realm's receipt invokes no graph, pane or template** (L1 §4 `C-10`).

## 8. The client store proxy — the data verbs, and the registration verbs refused

- **The client ctx's `store` member is a proxy over `ClientStoreVerb`** — `read`, `readLiteral`, `readTiered`, `write`, `delete`, `rollback`, `constraints`, `constraintOn`, `constraintOff` — **and NOT the full fifteen**: the six registration verbs (`listen`, `unlisten`, `register`, `removeListener`, `addConstraint`, `removeConstraint`) are the FOUNDATION's and are **REFUSED BY NAME, `client-registration-refused`**, as a value (`D-105`), because a client half that could `register` at a harness-known handle would replace its own main half's body behind admission's comparison (`C-3`, `S-3`, `ASM-MC-1`).
- **Under two realms the proxy sends a `StoreRequest` over the transport and answers a `Promise` of the `StoreAnswer`'s receipt**; the channel executes the act (the facade's own verb) and the turn/flush is the channel's. **Under one realm (a realm-hosted store) the proxy wraps the store's own answers in resolved Promises, so the module's `await` is the same under both topologies** (L1 §4 `C-13`, `S-13b`). **The answer is the store's own** — a receipt or a refusal — and the proxy mints no shape beside it (L1 §4 `C-2`, `D-311`).

## 9. Assumptions, each with its rejected alternative

- **`ASM-MC-1` — the client proxy exposes the DATA verbs and refuses the six registration verbs by name.** Alternative rejected: the full fifteen, which would let a client half replace a registered body at a handle admission never saw and make `C-3`'s one-handle grain a convention (`S-3`, `C-3`).
- **`ASM-MC-2` — the `reentrant-call` flag has ONE source, the binding's run, and ONE reader, `module-system`'s ctx.** Alternative rejected: a second flag at the ctx, which would let a main half's store verb answer a different refusal from the one the binding raised (`C-2`).
- **`ASM-MC-3` — the store act is the channel's own request/answer envelope over the one `S-12` member.** Alternative rejected: a second bridge member or a third `ChannelMessage` kind, both refused by `C-15`'s one-member/two-kind rule; and refusing a client store act under Electron, which would make `D-311`'s writer claim false in the only topology this tree boots.
- **`ASM-MC-4` — the client registry is keyed `{module, listener}` and the fan-out is one call per module.** Alternative rejected: a per-listener call, which would let a module's halves observe one turn out of order and would put containment below the module (L1 §3 `S-13`, findings 14).
- **`ASM-MC-5` — the entry carries the WRITE's address (`address?`), because the subscription relation compares it with the listener's DECLARED address.** Alternative rejected: deriving the write's address from the listener's own declaration, which is false for a leaf subscriber and a root write; and dropping the relation, which would make `C-3`'s subscription arm unmeasurable (`M-13`). **`C-2`'s five-arm entry shape is re-grained by this row** — one optional member, not a sixth arm.
- **`ASM-MC-6` — the client half is fired with the ENTRY, not with `data` alone.** Alternative rejected: firing with the raw `data`, which would hide the arm from the half and make `M-11`'s witness — *"the `turn` and each entry's `{module, listener, outcome, data}`"* — impossible to read; **`module-system`'s `fire(handle, data, address)` is re-grained to `fire(handle, entry)` in the same landing**, a cross-seat L4 edit declared there and not here (L1 §6, L2 §3).
- **`ASM-MC-7` — the declaration's handle is keyed by its `client` when it names one and by its address otherwise, so the store's entry and the realm's `DeliveryHandle` are ONE identity.** Alternative rejected: two keyings (the defect this cell corrects), which would make the fan-out fire a handle the realm never registered (`C-3`, `D-307`).
