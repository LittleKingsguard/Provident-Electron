## 1. The state, settled first: a MEMBER, and "by default" is a DERIVATION the record may overturn

- **THE QUESTION IS SETTLED BY ONE CONSEQUENCE: A PERSON WHO EXPANDS AN EMPTY ZONE MUST KEEP IT EXPANDED.** A derivation cannot remember a disagreement (`D-129`: a state the delivery cannot read is a state the next delivery overrules), and a zone-level control that presses into a derivation and changes nothing is the styled-dead control `D-235` made the corpus un-grey rather than explain. **So the state is a MEMBER** — `['zones', <id>, 'collapsed']`, an optional boolean at the family's own leaf — **and it is written by the ACTS and by nothing else.**
- **AND "BY DEFAULT" IS STILL NOT A WRITER, WHICH IS `D-243`'s RULE KEPT RATHER THAN BENT.** The member states nothing on a cold profile: `viewZonesPlaceholder` (`:304`) and `repairedZones` (`:1185`) write `size` and **no `collapsed`**, so the family's bytes do not move and `crossing.test.ts`'s pinned answer does not move — the same free widening `design-pane-collapse.md` §2 lands for the pane's `collapsed`. **The default is the PAINT's**: a zone nobody has spoken about presents collapsed **exactly when it holds no pane**, which is the user's *"an empty zone collapses by default"* read as the derivation it can only be, since **emptiness lives in the OTHER family and `D-186` forbids a repair to read `['zones']`** — the mirror holds: no repair reads `['panes']`.
- **ONE PURE PREDICATE ANSWERS IT, AND THREE READERS ASK IT** (`D-262`'s one-fact-four-readers discipline): **`zoneCollapsedOf(records, panes, zoneId)`** — `records`' record for `zoneId`: `collapsed === true` → **collapsed**; `=== false` → **expanded**; **absent → collapsed iff no pane in `panes` names that zone** (`pane.zone === zoneId`; the stage names no zone and counts for none, `docs/L1/app-layout.md` §4 `C-9`). Its three readers are the ZONE arm (§2), the PANE arm's effective collapse (§3, §8) and the zone control's toggle (§2). **The RECORD is the input and the TREE is not**: during a drag the preview re-parents real panes (`D-259`), so a tree-read derivation would answer a fact no release had made (`D-260`'s class, one family over).
- **AN EXPANDED EMPTY ZONE PERSISTS BECAUSE THE PRESS WRITES `false`, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT OF TAKING THE MEMBER.** The toggle negates the DERIVED answer, not the record's absent member (a toggle of `undefined` is a second default in the act): pressing a collapsed-by-default rail writes `collapsed: false`, the record now states the disagreement, and the next press writes `true`. **No press is a no-op** (`D-235`'s rule), on a cold profile or on the tenth boot.
- **THE OTHER EXTREME MEMBER IS NOT REUSED, AND ITS RESIDUE IS NAMED RATHER THAN ABSORBED** (`D-244` §5): `['zones', id, 'display']` (`'normal'`/`'minimized'`) already exists and `display: 'none'` is the FULLER minimize, written by an outside writer and offered no affordance. **A collapsed zone never writes `display`**, and a zone stating `display: 'none'` is hidden whole whatever its `collapsed` says — the two compose into ONE style value (§2) and neither is the other's spelling (`D-129`).

## 3. The collapsed extent: `auto`, and the ONE figure in it is the head row's own thickness

- **COLLAPSING NEVER TOUCHES THE `size`, AND THAT IS `D-234`'s REPAIR READ THE OTHER WAY.** The repair carries a stated figure and `repairedZones` spreads a standing record whole; **an act that cleared the figure would destroy the number a person set**, and the figure is precisely what rule 2's preview needs (§5). **So the member survives, unchanged and unread, while the zone is collapsed — it is the MEMORY an expansion restores with zero writes** (`design-pane-collapse.md` §3's own form, one family out).
- **THE COLLAPSED EXTENT IS NOT A CONSTANT AND IS NOT STORED: it is `auto` on the axis the zone fixes, and what resolves it is the zone's own content** (`LAYOUT_ZONE_AXES`' `member` column, `D-243`'s table read once). A declared `DEFAULT_ZONE_COLLAPSED_SIZE` was REFUSED: it would be a figure right for one pane count and false for another (§ below), and it would be a second authority beside the extent the cascade already computes (`D-129`, `D-174`).
- **THE ONE FIGURE IN THE BOX IS THE PANE HEAD'S THICKNESS, AND IT LIVES IN ONE PLACE**: `.pane-head > .pane-grab { height: 14px }` (`index.html:958`, `D-269`'s measured row). A collapsed `top`/`bottom` zone's HEIGHT is therefore `14px`, a collapsed `left`/`right` zone's WIDTH is `14px` (§4's rotation is what makes the second true), and **no record member and no `src/` byte states a figure the RECORD owns** — the `14px` is the sheet's own authored thickness, stated once, and it is not a zone's (`D-129`, `D-174`).
- **AND THE COUNT IS WHY IT CANNOT BE A CONSTANT** (the user's rule 3 read against rule 2): panes in one zone lie along the PANE-ADJUSTABLE axis (`D-258`'s axis-and-basis clause, `index.html:748`–`:750`), so **a zone of three panes pays ONE head row on the fixed axis and a zone of one pays the same** — while a zone of NONE pays **zero**: nothing resolves `auto`, and the empty rail takes no room, which is *"an empty zone collapses by default"* read as geometry rather than as prose. **The empty zone is still reachable**, because its control is a sibling with its own `14px` (§7, `D-244` §2's reason).

## 4. Heads in a collapsed zone, and what "vertically oriented" MEANS — and what it guarantees

- **A COLLAPSED ZONE SHOWS ITS PANES' HEADS BECAUSE NOTHING ELSE IS LEFT OF THEM, AND THE RULE THAT HIDES THE REST IS ALREADY WRITTEN.** The pane arm writes the effective collapse (§2), so `:has(> .pane-head)[collapsed="true"] > :not(.pane-head)` (§6, `:1011`'s rule with the scope rev 2 states) makes each pane its head row inside the collapsed zone too — **no new hide rule, no second spelling of one fact** (`D-129`). What the zone's own attribute buys is the extent (§3) and the two sheet rules below — never a third copy of the pane's state.
- **"ORIENTED VERTICALLY FOR SIDEBARS" IS A ROTATED HEAD LANE, AND THE ALTERNATIVES LOSE FOR A MEASURED REASON.** A `top`/`bottom` collapsed zone stacks its heads ALONG the arrangement axis already (a row), so each head keeps its natural `14px` thickness and rule 3 costs nothing. A `left`/`right` zone fixes the WIDTH and arranges along the HEIGHT — so a horizontal head inside it would set the collapsed WIDTH to the widest head's own content width (measured precedent: `[0,190,105,14]` inside a `105` content box, `D-257`), i.e. a `120px` rail would collapse to `~105px`: **the collapse would reclaim almost none of the only axis it can reclaim.** So the head row takes `writing-mode: vertical-rl` inside a collapsed sidebar zone, the label runs DOWN the lane, and the collapsed WIDTH becomes the head's thickness — the same `14px` a `top` zone pays on its height. **A COLUMN OF HEADS AT THEIR NATURAL HEIGHT WAS REFUSED ON THAT MEASUREMENT, and a narrow lane with unrotated text was refused because a `nowrap` name cannot fit a `14px` lane at all.**
- **WHAT THE DESIGN GUARANTEES ABOUT A ROTATED CONTROL — FOUR THINGS THAT ARE BOX ARITHMETIC AND NOT A HOPE, AND ONE THING IT WILL NOT GUARANTEE** (`D-178` says the felt behaviour is unverifiable here, so the design guarantees what it can state): **(i) NOTHING PRESSABLE ROTATES**: the rule applies to `.pane-head`, and `.pane-head > .pane-grab` and `.pane-collapse` take `writing-mode: horizontal-tb` — a square rotated is the same square, so every control's `14×14` box, and therefore its hit area, is INVARIANT — **rotation can cost the label's legibility and never a control's box**. **(ii) NOTHING IS CLIPPED WHILE THE HEADS FIT**: the zone's arrangement axis is `hidden` (§2) and the heads of a zone whose panes fit are laid inside it, so every head is reachable — **and where they DO NOT fit, §11's clipping takes a head, and that is the one pressability the design does not guarantee** (the zone's own control is a sibling and is never clipped, so the escape is one press). **(iii) THE LANE'S CROSS EXTENT IS THE GRIP'S OWN `14px`**, so the zone's `auto` resolves to a number the sheet already states. **(iv) THE TWO EXTENTS THE BASE RULE STATES ARE RE-STATED IN THE ROTATED FORM** (`writing-mode: vertical-rl` makes `width` the inline axis and `height` the block axis, so the grip's `height: 14px` becomes the cross extent and its `min-width: max-content` a floor along the lane): the base rule's axes are NOT reused, the pair is written where the rotation is (§6), and **the ORDER is the grip first, the name between, the control last along the lane** — the `top`/`bottom` order, with `D-269` §3's overflow lesson (a title that covered its own control) carried as (iii)'s floor.
- **THE DEGRADATION IS STATED RATHER THAN DISCOVERED**: a realm that refuses `writing-mode` paints the head horizontally inside a `14px` lane, so the NAME clips while every control keeps its box and its press — the label's loss, the act's non-loss. **What is NOT claimed is legibility**: a rotated label's readability and the reach of a rotated glyph are readings of the rendered surface this corpus has no row for (`D-178`'s list, L1 §6(vi)), and rule 3's *"the pane headers are shown"* is claimed as NODES PRESENT AND PRESSED, never as a comfortable read.

## 5. The preview near a collapsed zone — "near" is the drop's own box, and the sliver hole is named

- **"NEAR" IS THE DROP'S OWN BOX AND NOTHING WIDER, AND IT IS COMPOSED FROM THE REMEMBERED `size`.** `paneMoveZoneFigures` (`:3160`) reads `['zones', <id>, 'size']` and `paneMoveDropBoxOf` (`:3187`) composes each drop's band from those four figures — **both UNCHANGED, because a collapse does not clear the figure** (§3). So a collapsed zone's drop is its FULL REMEMBERED BAND, and `highlight`'s `elementFromPoint` arm (`D-249`, `D-250`) resolves it exactly as it resolves an expanded zone. **No "vicinity" wider than a zone's own recorded band is invented**: the corpus measured what an invented grid does (`D-250` §1: a `50px` lane against a `115px` rail).
- **THE HOLE IN RULE 2, NAMED WHERE IT WOULD BITE.** A collapsed zone's PRESENTED extent is one head row (§3) and an EMPTY one's is `0` — so **a drop box composed from the presented extent would be a `14px` sliver in a `top` band and NOTHING AT ALL over an empty rail**: the pointer would have to enter a band it cannot see, and the one zone a person most wants to drop into (an empty rail, which rule 2 exists for) would be unhittable by construction. **The design closes it by composing the box from the MEMORY (§3) and by making the ZONE'S OWN EXTENT the preview's subject, not the drop's**: for the zone under the pointer the picture and the target are one band, and the band is never the sliver.
- **AND THE FOUR BANDS ARE THE RECORDED LAYOUT'S, NOT THE PRESENTED ONE — WHICH IS CHOSEN, AND ITS COST IS NAMED** (rev 2's correction of a claim rev 1 overreached): `paneMoveDropBoxOf` composes all four bands, **including the neighbours' edges**, from the four remembered `size`s, so on a profile where three zones are collapsed by derivation (`AZ-1`) the presented zones have shifted and the bands have not. **That is rule 2 read as a generous catch band**: a pointer over the stage within a collapsed rail's remembered extent belongs to that rail, its preview expands the rail to exactly that extent, and the picture is then true for the zone it names — while the alternative, bands composed from the PRESENTED extents, is the sliver above. **The cost, stated rather than discovered**: a drop inside a collapsed rail's remembered band but outside its `14px` present commits to that rail, and **the band is generous for a collapsed zone and exact for an expanded one** — one rule, no per-zone special case, and the `AZ-3` rows read the zone the release actually named.
- **THE PREVIEW EXPANDS THE ZONE ITSELF, AS A TRANSIENT, AND REVERTS WITH NOTHING REMEMBERED.** `PANE_MOVE_PREVIEW.zone` (`:3064`) gains the zone's own member: the writer states `${member}: ${remembered}px` beside the two outline declarations — **the same node class and the same inline mechanism `zonePreviewOf`'s size transient already uses** (`D-244` §4's `Z5`), so `paneMovePreviewClear` (`:3123`) removes it BY NAME on the release, the cancel, a new press and a move off the zone, and **a delivery would replace the zone's `css.style` whole anyway** (`D-234`, `D-138`'s declared transient). Nothing is written to any record by any move (`D-249`'s measured property), so **a non-committing end returns the zone to collapsed by the same total restore `D-260`/`D-263` gave the panes**: `highlight(null, null, restore)` clears the zone's declarations and `previewPlacement(null)` puts the panes back.
- **AND THE PANES PRESENT THE POST-COMMIT PICTURE, WHICH IS WHY THE PREVIEW ANSWERS THE ZONE TERM `false` FOR THE DROP'S ZONE** (rev 2: the override is of ONE TERM and not of the whole answer, which rev 1 got wrong). `drivePreviewPanePaints` (`:3715`) composes each pane's effective collapse as `record.collapsed === true || zoneCollapsedOf(...)` — **the pane's OWN member left standing, because a pane a person collapsed stays collapsed** (`D-269`) — and for panes standing in the zone the drop names, **the ZONE term is `false` and only that term**, because rule 4's first trigger makes it true on commit (§8), and a preview that showed the zone as a set of heads while the release expands it would be a picture of a state no press can produce (`D-263`'s own standard). **THE OPERAND IS DECLARED**: the turn takes a second parameter, the drop's zone id (`null` on every restoring turn), which is the second private arity this page changes and the one §2's `panePaintOf` does not carry. Its command carries the pane's `collapsed` attribute beside its style, so the box and the attribute cannot disagree mid-gesture; **the write guard stays the style's, so an attribute-only difference costs ONE write per zone transition and never one per move** (`D-263`'s measurement kept).

## 6. The sheet block, and the three rules it owes

- **THE ZONE'S OWN ATTRIBUTE AND THE PANE'S ARE ONE WORD, SCOPED PER NODE, AND THAT IS ONE AUTHORITY PER NODE RATHER THAN TWO** (`D-129`): `collapsed` means *"this box presents collapsed"* on whichever node carries it, and the sheet tells them apart by the structural test it already owns — **the body rule becomes `:has(> .pane-head)[collapsed="true"] > :not(.pane-head)`** (`:865`'s key, `D-270`'s own "the box that heads itself"), so a collapsed zone can never hide its panes; and **the glyph rule is scoped to the pane's own attribute** (`[collapsed="true"] > .pane-head > .pane-collapse::before`, `:1003`), so a zone's attribute cannot speak for a pane's glyph.
- **THE ROTATED LANE'S BLOCK, WRITTEN HERE BECAUSE `src/renderer/index.html` IS THIS MODULE'S OWN PATH** (rev 2: rev 1 named the shape and owed the block, which was unwritten work rather than a module boundary — `D-3`); **the selector names no zone class and no id, and it needs neither**: a collapsed zone's attribute reaches the PANE by `>`, and the pane's zone is the CLASS it inherits by position (`D-265`), so `[collapsed="true"] > .pane-in-left > .pane-head, [collapsed="true"] > .pane-in-right > .pane-head { writing-mode: vertical-rl; }` is the whole key — and it cannot match a pane's own attribute, whose children are a head and a body (`:772`–`:776`). **THE FOUR EXEMPTIONS AND THE TWO RE-STATED EXTENTS ARE ONE RULE**: the same pair's `> .pane-grab` and `> .pane-collapse` take `writing-mode: horizontal-tb; height: 14px;` with `min-width: max-content` as the inline floor (§4 (i)–(iv)), **because `vertical-rl` moves `width` to the inline axis and `height` to the block axis, so the base rule's own pair is NOT inherited** (`:940`–`:953`). **THE `top`/`bottom` ARM STATES NOTHING**: a collapsed `top`/`bottom` zone's heads are a row already, so its head keeps the base rule byte for byte (`D-269`). **And the hunk sits beside the sheet's collapse block** (`:976`–`:1036`), in the same edit as the zone control's own rules (§7's hunk, `app-envelope` rev 8 §11 `A-11`), so the lane and the control it carries are one landing.
- **THE CONTROL'S GLYPH IS THE SIBLING COMBINATOR'S, AND IT NEEDS NO ID** (§7): `.zone-collapse::before { content: '▾' }` and `[collapsed="true"] + .zone-collapse::before { content: '▸' }` — the control is its zone's FOLLOWING sibling, so the rule reads the zone's own painted attribute and nothing is written on the control (`D-269` §3's "no `content` on the node", one node out).

## 8. The two expand triggers — what each writes — and the nested control that is not a cycle

- **THE CHAIN STANDS AHEAD OF THE PLACEMENT'S NO-OP TEST, AND THAT ORDER IS THE FIX FOR RULE 4(a)'s ONE UNREACHABLE CASE** (rev 2: rev 1 stated the order of the two writes and not their place in the act). A drop into the collapsed zone the pane ALREADY names composes a placement that agrees with the record, and `paneMoveAct`'s `X3b` (`:2814`) returns `false` before any write — **so the zone would stay collapsed while the preview had already drawn it expanded**, which is the divergence `D-260` is named for. So `paneMoveAct` is: **`X2a` read the drop's zone's `zoneCollapsedOf`; if `true`, send `['zones', <zone>, 'collapsed'] = false` FIRST and only proceed if it answered `committed`; then `X3b`'s no-op test, which answers `true` when the placement agrees — because the zone's expansion WAS the act, and the preview already stands on a record that now agrees with it; then `X3`'s pane write as it stands.** A refusal at `X3b`'s place therefore writes nothing at the pane and leaves the zone open — consistent, retryable, and named.
- **TRIGGER (a), A DROP INTO A COLLAPSED ZONE, IS A CHAINED PAIR AND THE PAIR IS WHY IT IS CHAINED** (`paneMoveAct`, `:2787`). The relocation's own write is one whole-family value at `['panes']` (`D-259`) and the zone's member is another family at another tier — **no address carries both, so the corpus's "one write and not a pair" rule (`D-142`, `D-249` §3) is inapplicable rather than overruled**, and the pair is made safe by its ORDER and its GATE: **`['zones', <zone>, 'collapsed'] = false` FIRST, and the pane's whole-family write only if that answer is `committed`.** A refusal at the zone leaves the panes exactly where they stand — a person sees an open zone and an unmoved pane, and can retry; the inverse order would land a relocation nobody can see. **Both writes are `D-117`'s register**, and the act's `true` answer keeps its one meaning: the placement stands.
- **TRIGGER (b), "UNCOLLAPSING A PANE WITHIN THE ZONE", IS ONE LEAF AT THE ZONE'S OWN ADDRESS — AND THE GLYPH IS WHAT MAKES IT HONEST** (`installPaneCollapse`'s body, `:2858`). While its zone is collapsed, **a pane's painted `collapsed` is the EFFECTIVE one (§2)**, so its head reads `▸` and the press the person makes is an "open" press: the act reads the pane's `zone`, asks `zoneCollapsedOf`, and where the answer is `true` it writes **`['zones', <zone>, 'collapsed'] = false` and nothing at the pane's leaf** — because the presentation that must change is the zone's, and the pane's own member is memory this press did not speak about. **The consequence is named and it is not a dead press**: a pane that was ITSELF collapsed keeps its member, so after the zone expands its head reads `▸` again and the next press is the pane's own act (`design-pane-collapse.md` §1's `X3`). One control, one act, chosen by the state its own glyph already shows.
- **THE TWO CONTROLS ARE TWO BOXES AT TWO DEPTHS AND THERE IS NO CYCLE, STRUCTURALLY.** The zone's control is a SIBLING of the zone and reads `props.zone`; a pane's control is inside the pane's head and reads `props.pane` (`D-269` §2). The zone's act writes only `['zones']`, the pane's act writes `['panes']` unless (§ above) its zone is collapsed — **that branch is triggered by a READ of the zone's record and never by the pane's write**, so no act calls another, no delivery is required to settle a state, and each record remains the one authority over its own member (`D-129`). **`D-269`'s per-pane collapse and this per-zone collapse are two controls over nested boxes with two addresses and two tiers**, and the one place they meet is a presentation rule both sides can state.
- **AND `D-258`'s PROVISIONAL NOTE IS NARROWED, ONCE MORE, IN ONE CLAUSE** (`D-259` narrowed sequence, `design-pane-collapse.md` §3 narrowed basis): **while its zone is collapsed, a pane's stored figure is MEMORY and not its basis** — the pane is its head row — **and the AXIS clause and the BASIS clause stand untouched for every expanded zone.** **The SIZES' INTERACTION — a figure exceeding its zone, a pane added to a full one — stays owed to L1/L2**, and a collapsed zone is one more case of it, stated rather than discovered.
