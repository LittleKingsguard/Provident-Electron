No export, no route, no channel, no bridge member, no tier and no published name is added.

## 1. The gap, stated as the design's

- **THE RECORD ALREADY CARRIES THE FIGURE AND THE PAINT ALREADY OBEYS IT** (`D-234`): `['zones', <id>, 'size']` at `mem`, four declared ids, `DEFAULT_ZONE_SIZES` as the one source, the repair and the placeholder both written from it, and `driveLayoutRegions`' zone arm writing `${size}px` plus the zone's scroll column into ONE `css.style` value. **A zone's figure is therefore a record member with a writer in the store and a reader on the surface, and no producer between them** — `D-151`'s *"the zone family's `commitZones` has no UI caller at all"* is the same fact said from the route's side.
- **IT IS NOT A MISSING TURN ANYONE FORGOT TO WIRE**: the charter fixes the zones' dimensions and never says how a person sets one (`D-185`), so the design owed an affordance and had not decided one. This page decides it: **a strip per zone, on the edge that bounds the zone, dragging the one member the paint reads.**
- **AND THE ONE THING THE LANDED MACHINERY ALREADY GIVES AWAY**: `D-238` generalised the pane gesture from one element to a declared list so **a third handle costs one line**. That list's entries name a PANE (`paneId`) because its subject was a pane; the loop itself (`startGutterAffordance`'s `:612`) is a loop over *handles*, and the only pane-shaped thing every entry does is **derive which record the figure lands in** — so the second subject kind is an adapter at the loop's head, not a second gesture (`D-238`'s own reason, one subject over).

## 2. The four strips: where each stands, which axis it reads, which sign

| Zone | The edge it stands on | Why that edge | Node position | Pointer axis | `sign` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `top` | the zone's **BOTTOM** edge | the zone's `size` is the distance from the window's top to that edge, so the edge is what a person pushes | **the zone's FOLLOWING SIBLING** in the root column | `y` | `+1` |
| `bottom` | the zone's **TOP** edge | the zone's `size` is the distance from the window's foot UP to that edge | the zone's **PRECEDING SIBLING** in the root column | `y` | `-1` |
| `left` | the zone's **RIGHT** edge | the `size` is the distance from the window's left to that edge | the zone's **FOLLOWING SIBLING** in the arrangement row | `x` | `+1` |
| `right` | the zone's **LEFT** edge | the `size` is the distance from the window's right IN to that edge | the zone's **PRECEDING SIBLING** in the arrangement row | `x` | `-1` |

- **ONE RULE GENERATES THE TABLE, AND IT IS THE STAGE THAT NAMES THE EDGE**: every strip stands on the zone's **bounding edge, which is the edge facing the stage**, because that is the edge whose position IS the zone's figure (`D-185`). `top`/`left` grow as that edge moves toward increasing coordinate (`+1`); `bottom`/`right` grow as it moves toward decreasing (`-1`). **THE SIGN IS A COEFFICIENT AND NOT A TASTE** — `D-238`'s own form: one mapping, negated where the edge's sense inverts.
- **THE STRIP IS THE ZONE'S SIBLING AND NEVER ITS CHILD, AND THAT IS MEASURED RATHER THAN PREFERRED.** `paintStyle` hands the engine ONE `css.style` value in `replace` mode, so **a zone's authored style does not survive the first paint** — `D-234` measured exactly that on these four nodes (`height: 140px` and nothing else, the authored `overflow` gone) — which means a zone can carry **no `position: relative`** for an absolutely placed child and **no authored layout at all** for a flowed one. A strip inside the zone is therefore a strip whose placement the tree cannot state. **AND THE SECOND REASON IS STRUCTURAL: a zone's figure can reach `0`** (§5), and a handle inside a zero-sized zone is a handle no press can land on — the `14px`-strip argument (`D-194`, `OWED-1`'s taken figure) turned into a recovery requirement. **A SIBLING KEEPS ITS OWN `14px` WHATEVER THE ZONE'S FIGURE IS**, so a zone dragged to nothing is a zone dragged back.
- **AND THAT IS ALSO WHAT KEEPS THE STRIPS OUT OF THE PANE HANDLES' PROBLEM**: the two landed handles are authored INSIDE painted nodes (`#gutter-horizontal` inside `#gutter-pane`, `#menus-vertical` inside `#pane-menus`), so **their edge placement is lost with the parent's style** — the same collision `D-234` routed and `D-243` fixed for the fill, read off the same two sites in this seat and **NOT measured in a renderer here**: L1 §6(vi) already declares the edge unreadable, and this page declines to add four more nodes to that list. **The strips' own `css.style` is never painted** — the paint's subjects are the four zones, the panes and the rows' targets (`reportMeasuredFigures`' own subject list) — so a strip's authored placement is stable by construction.
- **`props.axis` IS THE STRIP'S OWN ID AND THE CURSOR COMES FROM THE PRIVATE MAP** (`D-193`'s form, one node shape): `css.id` = `props.id` = `props.axis` = the one string, `css.classes: ['gutter-handle']` as the shared styling hook, `content: ''`, and the ONE authored `pointerdown` body the gutter handle carries, shared, writing nothing (`D-177`: `list_targets`-visible and `dispatch`-reachable). `GUTTER_CURSORS` gains four keys — `row-resize` for the two horizontal strips, `col-resize` for the two vertical ones — **and that map is private, so no export joins the surface** (`D-192`'s own arithmetic: the strip ids are LITERALS on both sides, the zone ids' precedent, and the equality is a row and never a derivation, `D-188` debt 2).
- **NO AUTHORED `resizable` IS NEEDED, AND THE REASON IS THE EXAMPLE'S**: the affordance's veto seam reads the PARENT element's `resizable` attribute (`gutterSeamExample().resizableOf`, `:358`) and an absent attribute is the demo's own *"no veto"* reading — so a strip whose parent is `app-surface` or the arrangement is resizable by the declaration that already stands, and the envelope authors nothing for it.

## 3. The stub L4 receives — the ONE new declaration, and the adapter at the loop's head

```
interface ZoneGestureAddress {           // the zone's counterpart of `PaneGestureAddress` (`:377`), same three ideas, one more field
  readonly handleId: string              // the authored strip's own id — also the gesture key every seat of THIS strip is handed
  readonly zoneId: string                // the RECORD's declared zone id — `DECLARED_ZONE_IDS`' member, never the node's id spelling
  readonly axis: 'x' | 'y'               // the POINTER coordinate this strip reads: `y` for top/bottom, `x` for left/right
  readonly sign: 1 | -1                  // +1 where the bounding edge moves toward increasing coordinate, -1 where it moves back
  readonly targetId: string              // the ZONE node the inline preview is written on — the strip's subject, not a fixed target
}

const ZONE_DRAG_HANDLES: readonly ZoneGestureAddress[] = [
  { handleId: 'zone-top-horizontal',    zoneId: 'top',    axis: 'y', sign:  1, targetId: 'top'    },
  { handleId: 'zone-bottom-horizontal', zoneId: 'bottom', axis: 'y', sign: -1, targetId: 'bottom' },
  { handleId: 'zone-left-vertical',     zoneId: 'left',   axis: 'x', sign:  1, targetId: 'left'   },
  { handleId: 'zone-right-vertical',    zoneId: 'right',  axis: 'x', sign: -1, targetId: 'right'  },
]

interface GestureSubject { readonly kind: 'pane' | 'zone'; readonly id: string }   // what a handle's figure lands in
interface GestureAddress { handleId: string; subject: GestureSubject; axis: 'x' | 'y'; sign: 1 | -1; targetId: string }

function gestureAddressesOf(): readonly GestureAddress[]   // THE ADAPTER: `PANE_DRAG_HANDLES` byte-unchanged, mapped verbatim
function zoneExtentOf(axis: 'x' | 'y'): number              // §5's clamp ceiling — ONE private total read, both axes
```

- **`PANE_DRAG_HANDLES` IS NOT RE-GRAINED, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE**: its entries, its `sign` and its missing axis member are `D-238`'s landed design and `PS-1`…`PS-4`'s subject; the adapter maps each entry to `{kind:'pane', id: paneId}`, `axis:'x'` (**the example's own `pointer.x - start` mapping, `:345`, byte-identical in effect to today's `sign === 1 ? example : -example`**) and `targetId: GUTTER_TARGET_ID` (**the composition's one target, `D-144`**). So the pane arm's reading does not move and the zone arm is the only new behaviour.
- **THE ZONE MAPPING READS `y` STRUCTURALLY, BECAUSE THE SEAM'S TYPE DOES NOT DECLARE IT** (`:298` types the pointer `{ readonly x: number }`): the wiring's own closure reads `pointer.y` through a `typeof === 'number'` gate and answers `Number.NaN` otherwise — **the example's own degradation** (`:346` returns `Number.NaN` for a non-number, and the affordance's `M1`-shaped gate makes the move INVALID rather than writing a figure). **No other module's file moves**: widening a seam type in `src/shared/gutter-affordance.ts` would be a second owner's hunk for a member the runtime already carries (`pointerOf` answers `{x, y}`).

## 4. The turn: three seams and ONE write, at `mem`, and NO `['drag']`

```
per gesture, on `zone-top-horizontal` (every strip is the same five lines):
Z1  startSizeOf(zoneId)      = zoneOf(zoneId)?.size            // THE SHAPE's read (`view-client.ts:563`); `undefined` where the family states no figure — and NO default (§5)
Z2  defaultSizeFor           = Z1's answer                      // the affordance's own seam, exactly as the pane handle takes it (`D-168`: one read, one name)
Z3  boundsOf(zoneId)         = { min: 0, max: zoneExtentOf(axis) }   // §5: finite in EVERY realm, never `undefined` (`D-238`'s NaN lesson)
Z4  sizeFromPointer(pointer, start) = sign === 1 ? mapped(axis) : -mapped(axis)   // mapped = `pointer[axis] - start`, the example's expression
Z5  applyPreview(state)      = the INLINE transient on the ZONE element: `style.setProperty(<the zone's member>, value + 'px')`
                               // a rendered element write and NEVER a graph write — `applyPreview`'s own class, `D-138`'s P1–P5 shape
Z6  commit(gesture, value)   = view.commitZones(zoneId, { size: value })   // ONE route, ONE member, ONE leaf: `['zones', zoneId, 'size']` (`D-152`)
Z7  move / cancel            = NOTHING IS WRITTEN: the strip's transient is Z5's and the record's only writer is Z6
```

- **THE ACT, THE TIER, AND THE ADDRESS, ONCE EACH** (`D-185`, `D-151`, `D-152`): the act is `commitZones(<zoneId>, { size })`; it lands as ONE LEAF at `['zones', <zoneId>, 'size']` because that route writes one leaf per supplied member; and **the tier is the family's own, `mem`** (brief §2's families table, `D-91`) — **no caller states a tier, because a drag does not choose one**.
- **IT RIDES NO `['drag']` FAMILY, AND THERE ARE THREE REASONS** (`D-145`, `D-243`, `D-129`): (i) `['drag']` is keyed by PANE ID and is GESTURE-BLIND, and the ghost turn's predicate reads `branch[DEFAULT_PANE_ID]` (`renderer.ts:4480`) — a zone key in that family would make ONE declared transient carry two subject kinds and the ghost's predicate would answer for the wrong one; (ii) what `['drag']` buys the pane gesture is a GRAPH-declared transient (`data-ghost`), while the zone's transient is a figure that the ZONE's own inline style can show at the same instant (**Z5**), so no second transient is owed; (iii) a zone key would be a `store-view-record` member and address change for a transient, and `D-145` declares that family **UNPAIRED** — no invariant holds it, so nothing repairs it and nothing should grow into it. **SO THE ZONE GESTURE HAS EXACTLY ONE WRITE, AND IT IS THE `file`-CLASS ONE**: `mem`, at release.
- **THE PREVIEW IS A RENDERED WRITE AND THE PAINT WILL OVERWRITE IT, WHICH IS THE DECLARED TRANSIENT'S OWN CONTRACT** (`D-138`, `D-132`): the paint replaces a zone's `css.style` whole on the next delivery, so a preview that outlives its gesture is replaced by the record's figure — **a stale preview is never fabricated and never persisted**.
- **EVERY REFUSAL IS READ, NEVER SWALLOWED** (`D-117`'s register, and `N1`'s sibling for the synchronous statement): `commitZones` answers a STATEMENT, so `{status:'absent'}` is `console.warn` with its `reason` verbatim; the receipt for a sent write is the push's (`onWriteOutcome`), which already reports the `zone` route — **and that is the ONE arm this page revives**: `reportReceipt` skips `zone` today with the reason *"the zone family has no UI caller at all"* (`renderer.ts:4199`–`:4202`). **That sentence becomes false at this rev, so it is re-grained in the same edit**: the `zone` route joins `pane`/`drag` in that filter, `refused` → `console.error`, `unread` → `console.warn`.

## 5. The clamp — and why it is FINITE in every realm, and why `0` is lawful

- **THE CEILING IS THE WINDOW'S OWN EXTENT ON THE AXIS THE ZONE FIXES**: `zoneExtentOf('y')` is the realm's `window.innerHeight`, `zoneExtentOf('x')` its `innerWidth`, censused structurally once per gesture (the `W1` idiom: a realm that does not answer a finite positive number gets the degradation, never a throw). **A zone can never exceed the window it stands in, which is the "bounded by the window" half in its only measurable form.**
- **THE DEGRADATION IS THE RECORD'S OWN FIGURE AND IT IS THE HONEST DIRECTION**: where the realm answers no finite positive extent — the layout-less DOM shim, which is the whole unit suite's realm (`D-27`) — the read answers **the zone's current `size`**, so the gesture can only SHRINK what it cannot measure. **A NaN here is what made the menus handle inert with the wiring looking correct** (`D-238`: `paneBoundsOf` answered `undefined`, `clampToBounds` turned it into `NaN`, every move was invalid) — so the arm that must not exist is the one that answers `undefined`.
- **"WHAT THE OTHER THREE LEAVE" IS NOT A CLAMP THIS DESIGN INVENTS** (`D-26`, `D-240`'s precedent): the arrangement is a **flex layout whose remainder is the stage** (`D-185`), so two zones that together exceed the window collapse the stage to `0` and each zone keeps its own `overflow` (`D-234`'s column) — **the honest failure is the one `D-240` already chose for the bar: clip, rather than hide a control.** A stage MINIMUM would be a figure somebody must MEASURE before this corpus may state it, so it is named as an OWED decision (§7) and never picked here.
- **THE FLOOR IS `0`, AND THAT IS WHAT MAKES A ZONE REACHING ZERO LAWFUL**: the record already carries a `0` **minimize marker** — `D-234`'s repair *"carries every zone that states its own figure ... and even a `0` minimize marker survive[s]"*, and the paint's own gate admits `size >= 0` (`:2206`) — so **a drag CAN produce `0`, and a `0`-sized zone is a record the design already admits rather than a state the gesture must forbid.** What it renders is `${member}: 0px` plus its scroll column: a zone of no extent, present in the graph and in the markup, whose content the browser clips — **and recoverable, because the strip is its sibling (§2)**. **`display: 'none'` remains the FULLER minimize** (the record's other member, read by the zone arm, `:2218`) and **no affordance is offered for it**: it stays an outside writer's, `D-151`'s residue, and inventing a second control for one act would be two affordances for one member.

## 6. What the strips do not touch — the pane's handle, and the pane's figure

- **THE TWO AFFORDANCES CANNOT MEET, AND THE GUARANTEES ARE STRUCTURAL** (all four, so no arm rests on a pixel): **different nodes** (`#zone-…` against `#gutter-horizontal`/`#menus-vertical`); **different containers** (the strips are the zones' siblings in the root column and the arrangement row, the pane handles are inside panes); **different axes** (a strip moves the axis its zone FIXES, a pane handle the axis its pane ADJUSTS — `C-11`'s complement, so the pair is orthogonal by construction); and **different cursors** (`row-resize`/`col-resize` on a strip against a pane handle's own token). The nearest their boxes can come is **a corner where the arrangement's row meets the top zone's strip**, and there the strip's own box wins because it is the later sibling in markup order — **stated, because it is the one place a press could be ambiguous.**
- **NO PANE FIGURE IS READ, WRITTEN OR CLAMPED BY A ZONE GESTURE**: a strip's subject is a ZONE id, its figure is `['zones', id, 'size']`, and its clamp is the window's — `D-243`'s own distinction kept exactly: **the fill is a layout relation, `bounds` is the drag's pixel clamp, and a zone's extent never becomes a pane's ceiling** (nor the reverse: `D-186`'s *"no repair reads `['zones']`"* stands).
- **`zoneSizeConstraint`/`zoneSizeRepair` ARE NOT REUSED, AND THE REFUSAL IS NAMED** (`:3986`/`:4015`; L2 §2's row): their domain is `{min … max} ∪ {0}` for a CALLER's own closure and their subject is the pane's adjustable member (their `src/` names stand, `:23` of L2's page). `zoneSizeConstraint(0, extent)` would spell this clamp a second time in a second mechanism with **no instrument to catch the two drifting** — the class `D-243` refused when it read `LAYOUT_ZONE_AXES` instead of declaring a table; the gesture's clamp is `boundsOf` and it is one.

## 8. Guardrail notes — what L4 must NOT do

- **Do not write a zone's `css.style` from the gesture**: the painter owns that key whole (`D-234`, `D-243`), so the preview is an INLINE element write and the durable write is the record's leaf — a graph write of the style would put two authors on one attribute (`D-129`).
- **Do not invent a default, a fallback or an authored height for a zone** (`D-174`, `D-191`'s `OWED-4` answer): a `size` the record does not state is written NOTHING, and the gesture's `Z1` absence arm answers no figure rather than a remembered one.
- **Do not hand `clampToBounds` an `undefined`, and do not let the extent read answer `NaN`** (`D-238`): the failure is an INERT handle that looks wired, and `ZS-3` exists for it.
- **Do not generalise `PANE_DRAG_HANDLES`' entries** (`D-238`, `PS-1`…`PS-4`): the adapter stays at the loop's head; the pane list's members, its `sign` and its absent axis are a landed measurement.
- **Do not add a route, a channel, a bridge member, a tier, an export or a second `['drag']` key** (`D-151`, `D-18`, `D-123`, `D-145`): the write is `view.commitZones`'s existing one, and `['zones']`'s tier is the family's.
- **Do not spell a zone or strip id twice where a row can compare them** (`D-188` debt 2, `D-41`): the envelope's literals and this table's literals are TWO spellings by design, and the equality is `ZS-1`'s reading plus `AL-10`'s for the record's side.
- **Do not claim a rendered surface for any of it** (`D-27`, `D-178`): the strips' presence is markup, their EDGE is authored `css`, and a unit green here is envelope-green.
