## 1. The first thing to settle: the bar must exist in EVERY zone, and that decides everything after it

- **WHAT `D-257` BUILT, MEASURED.** The header row is **the `.pane-grab` grip itself** — `width: auto !important`, `display: flex`, its name an `::after { content: attr(name) }` — and every rule that makes it a row is keyed on **the inherited `pane-in-left`/`pane-in-right` classes** (`index.html:837`–`:864`, `D-265`'s binding). **A `top`/`bottom` pane therefore has NO header at all**: the grip takes `float: left; position: sticky; left: -0.875rem` (`menusGrabStyle`, `:985`) and stands *inside* the menus' own line, and its `::after` is never given a `content`.
- **A CONTROL BAR CARRYING A COLLAPSE CONTROL AND AN INDICATOR CANNOT BE SIDEBAR-ONLY**, and the design refuses the two cheaper shapes on measured grounds rather than taste. **(a) THE COLLAPSE CONTROL CANNOT GO INSIDE THE GRIP, IN ANY ZONE**: a `pointerdown` on any descendant of `.pane-grab` **bubbles to the grip's own listener and ARMS the pane-move session** (`installPaneMoveDrag`, `D-249` §1), and **that same press is `D-260`'s reveal arm** — it writes `visibility: visible` on the four drops (`index.html:1025`–`:1034`), so one click on the control would arm a drag and paint four zone windows. **`D-260` is cited because it corrects the obvious reading**: focus ALONE no longer shows a drop (`opacity: 0; visibility: hidden` is the closed state, `:1029`–`:1034`), so the objection is the PRESS and the session it arms — **and it is named precisely**: a click with no travel commits nothing (`travelArmed`, so the session ends without a write), while the four windows and the armed session both still happen. **(b) THE BAR CANNOT STAY THE GRIP WITH THE TWO CONTROLS BESIDE IT**, because the pane is a flex COLUMN in a sidebar (`index.html:775`–`:782`) and two siblings are laid out down it, not along it — **and that second ground is the one that decides the row**: a control bar whose two controls the user's own sentence puts ON the bar needs a box that lays them along the row (`D-257`'s *"the grip IS the header row"* stops being enough). **So the bar is a ROW NODE, and the grip becomes its first child** (§2) — forced by (b) and made unambiguous by (a).
- **WHAT A `top`/`bottom` PANE PAYS FOR THE ROW, AND WHY THE FILL ABSORBS IT.** `D-243` writes `height: 100%` there — a RELATION against the zone's own extent — so the row is laid out **inside the pane's content box** and **no record byte and no zone figure moves**: the zone keeps its `size` (`D-233`), the pane keeps its adjustable `width`, and the row comes out of the content's height. **THE FILL IS EXACTLY WHAT PAYS FOR IT**, and this is the one place `D-257`'s sidebar trade (a lane for a row) inverts: in `top`/`bottom` the row is paid by the fill and the pane keeps its lane.

## 3. The three re-parents, and the pins they release

- **THE PIN MOVES FROM THE GRIP TO THE ROW, AND THE LANE GOES WITH IT.** `D-246` pinned the grip because it was floated inside a scroller and every descendant of a scroll container travels with it; **the row is not floated and its width is the scrollport's** (`width: auto` on a block-level box resolves against the scroll container's content box), so on the axis a pane does not scroll it cannot travel — **and because a `top`/`bottom` pane DOES scroll (`overflow-x`, `D-243`), the row takes BOTH sticky insets: `top: 0` for the sidebar arm and `left: 0` for the horizontal one.** `D-246`'s inset was `-0.875rem` to escape the grip's lane; with the lane gone the inset is `0`, so the row satisfies `D-246`'s rule in the zone it was measured in without a zone-keyed rule (§6, `design-pane-collapse.md`). `menusGrabStyle` then loses `float: left`, `position: sticky` and `left: -0.875rem`, `inspectorGrabStyle` the same three, and `gutterGrabStyle` loses `position: relative` and `align-self: flex-start` — **the head row is `position: sticky`, and the box that makes the stacking context** (`D-261`'s rank, `D-246`'s class). The lane the inset was buying is returned twice: `#pane-menus > #menus-grab`'s `margin-left: -0.875rem` (`index.html:624`) and `:where(#pane-menus)`'s `padding-left: 0.875rem` (`:608`) go in the same edit, and `:where(#pane-inspector)`'s pair (`:666`–`:667`) with them.
- **THE THREE `!important`s THAT EXISTED ONLY TO BEAT AN INLINE `css` ARE NOW ORDINARY.** `width: auto !important`, `align-self: stretch !important`, `position: sticky !important` on `.pane-grab` were fighting `#gutter-grab`'s authored `position: relative`/`align-self: flex-start` (`:815`–`:822`, `D-257`'s own finding at that block). **The row is a node that authors no `style`** (§2), so its box is the sheet's without a fight — and the grip, now a flex ITEM of that row, needs none of the three (`D-257`'s `!important` trio is deleted with its cause, `D-70`'s dead-declaration discipline).
- **`#pane-menus`'s `padding-right: 1.125rem` STAYS**: it is the resize strip's lane, not the grip's (`D-253`), and `D-261`'s rank on the strip is untouched. **`D-257`'s measured round trip is NOT preserved and the landing pass owes a re-measure**: the sidebar's header row moves from the grip's own box to the row's — same `14px` band, same width, one box over (`§8`).

## 4. The collapse control's one owner per fact, and the glyph's single author

- **THE NODE AUTHORS NO GLYPH AND NO STATE; THE SHEET AUTHORS THE GLYPH.** The glyph is one static character, but **a `content` on the node and a `::before` on it would be TWO owners of one box** — the exact class `D-129` refuses — so the node carries `content` from nobody and the sheet paints `▸`/`▾` under `[collapsed="true"]` and its absence (`design-pane-collapse.md` §6). The one static string the envelope keeps is the grip's `⠿` (`:958`, the `+`'s case, `D-239`).
- **IT AUTHORS `props.pane` AND NOTHING ELSE BESIDES ITS ID AND ITS PAIR** (§2): the operand is DATA with a node id in the address book, so the act's census in the renderer is three NODE ids and **no record id is spelled there** — the eight drops' own shape (`D-248`, `D-235`), and the reason `C2` of the act can forbid a table in the renderer at all.
- **AND ITS `tabindex: '0'` COSTS NOTHING BECAUSE IT IS THE GRIP'S SIBLING**: the reveal it must not trigger is the grip's `:focus-within` — which `D-260` emptied of effect — and the drag it must not start is the grip's own listener, which no sibling can reach (`§1`, `D-249`).

## 5. The indicator: a pseudo-element, and why that is lawful HERE

- **A PSEUDO-ELEMENT CANNOT RECEIVE A CLICK, AND THAT IS THE RULE THAT DECIDES THIS SECTION.** A pointer event on a `::before`/`::after` box is dispatched to its ORIGINATING ELEMENT, so a pseudo is **not addressable** — `provident.dispatch`, `list_targets` and a row's own id read cannot reach it and no handler pair can hang on it (`AGENTS.md`'s UI authority). **So the COLLAPSE CONTROL must be a node (§2) and the INDICATOR may be a pseudo**: the indicator is read, never pressed.
- **AND IT MUST NOT SIT ON THE GRIP FOR THE `attr()` REASON `D-257` ALREADY MEASURED**: `attr()` reads the attribute of the element the pseudo belongs to, so the indicator's carrier is the head row, which the renderer writes `props.status` onto — **one attribute, one writer, one reader, and no node**. A grip-borne indicator would have needed a second writer on the grip, which is the node `paintStyle` must never touch (`D-265`: the pane's and the grip's `props` are untouched so `props.name` survives).
- **ABSENCE PAINTS NOTHING, AND THAT IS `D-243`'s NO-DEFAULT RULE APPLIED TO A VIEW.** The sheet styles the pseudo **under the HEAD's own `[status]`** — `.pane-head[status]::after { content: attr(status) }`, the attribute the head arm writes on the head node and `attr()` therefore reads off it (`D-257`'s binding, one node over) — so a record naming no status has no rule, no `content` and no box; **an app that does not use indicators says so by not stating the member**, which is the user's own *"optional, based on app's use case"*.

## 8. Guardrail notes — what L4 must NOT do

- **DO NOT PUT THE COLLAPSE CONTROL INSIDE `.pane-grab`, IN ANY ZONE** (§1): its `pointerdown` bubbles to the grip's drag listener and `D-260`'s press writes the four drops visible — a drag and four windows from one click. **This is the reason the row exists**, and it is the drag and not the focus that carries it.
- **DO NOT AUTHOR A `css.style` ON THE HEAD ROW** (§2): the row is not painted, and a `position`/`display` here would race the sheet for a box that no record owner claims (`D-129`, `D-174`).
- **DO NOT AUTHOR A GLYPH ON THE COLLAPSE NODE** (§4): the sheet's `::before` is the one owner; a node `content` beside it renders too and the two disagree the moment the state changes (`D-129`).
- **DO NOT LEAVE ONE PANE'S BODY IN ITS OWN `content`** (§2, `A-14`): a node's text renders ahead of its children and no child rule can hide it, so a collapsed pane would keep opening to its body's height (`D-259`'s measurement, one state over).
- **DO NOT EXPORT THE SEVEN IDS, AND DO NOT DERIVE ONE FROM A PANE ID** (`§6`, `D-41`, `D-188` debt 2): the equality between a node id and its record pane is a ROW's, never a string operation — a new published name moves that map row's `endpoints` and this rev publishes none.
- **DO NOT RE-AUTHOR THE GRIP'S `props`, ITS GLYPH OR ITS FOUR DROPS** (§2, `D-248`): the drop ids, their `props.pane`/`props.zone` operands and the reveal mechanism are unmoved; the re-parent moves the node and nothing in it.
- **DO NOT GIVE THE INDICATOR A NODE TO PROVE IT EXISTS** (§5): a node would need a reader and a paint write of its own (`D-174`: a value nothing reads is debt), and the pseudo's value is already in the markup as the attribute `get_rendered_html` emits (`D-257`'s own argument).
- **DO NOT TOUCH THE `zone-format` BINDING** (`:749`–`:767`): the head row inherits its zone's form class by position like every descendant; a fourth pane still needs no CSS edit (`D-265`).
