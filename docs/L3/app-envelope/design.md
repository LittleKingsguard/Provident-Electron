## 1. What this rev is, and the shape of the whole delivery

- **ONE FILE, ONE TREE, AND EVERY NODE IN IT NAMES ITS READER IN ONE LINE** (`D-177`): a node whose reader cannot be written is not authored, which is why the arrangement container and the stage carry no id (§2), why the eighteen items carry no id (their role is read off the node), and why the four region ids are literals rather than five more exports (§9).
- **THE TREE'S TOPOLOGY IS THE CHARTER'S, AND THE ORDER IS THE ONE L2 §2 FIXES** (`D-176`, `C-5`): `app-surface`'s children are **the top-bar → the top zone → the arrangement container → the bottom zone → the opener**, and the container holds `left` + the stage + `right` in a row, the sidebars stretching between the two full-width zones. `template.root`'s children stay **`app-surface`, then the overlay layer, then the row prototype** — the layer keeps its adjacency to the root so the scrim still covers the viewport, and the prototype stands outside the subtree the modal writes `inert` to (`D-113`, `D-178` §3).
- **THE COUNT, TAKEN HERE AND OWED NOWHERE** (`D-26`): **35 new authored nodes at rev 6** (**34** at rev 5 — the bar, the tabs row, the prototype, four zones, the container, the stage, the menus pane, its handle, four menus, eighteen items, the receipt carrier — plus rev 6's measured-figures carrier; see §2's table), **SEVEN existing nodes re-parented** (the h1, the four content cards, the two terminals), and **two nodes edited in place** (the gutter handle, `app-surface`'s `css.style`). **The pages above count FOUR re-parented; the design counts seven** because the stage's children are the two terminals (L2 §2) and the demo `h1` is a node neither page places (§8).

## 6. What this module may not claim (`D-177`, `D-178`, `D-182`)

- **The node list is STATIC**: no `each`, no `repeat`, no per-node family binding, no conditional — and **`derived` carries `props`/`css` ONLY, never `content`**. So every label here is AUTHORED (the static strings §2 lists) or PAINTED by a turn, and **not one is computed in the envelope**.
- **NO node may be documented as "placed into a zone at runtime"**: placement routing does not work post-bootstrap (a mint dirties only its target, the container is never recompiled, path-compiling double-emits — upstream-owned, `D-5`/`D-52`). **A minted row renders wherever its TARGET lives**, the pane is authored where it belongs, and the `zone` member is DECLARATIVE (`C-8`, `C-9`).
- **No authored figure a record owns, and no authored fallback**: no zone dimension, no zone `display`, no pane `width`/`height`, no stage dimension, and no string standing in for a missing answer (`D-185`, `D-189`, `D-129`, `D-174`).
- **No window control is authored and there is no dispatchable close** — the three buttons are the platform's, in a region the page does not own (`D-178` §2, `D-179`).
