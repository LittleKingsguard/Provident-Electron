## 1. The defect, in the store's own words, and the one sentence that decides the design

- **`D-218` measured the silence and `D-219` measured its cause away.** `1.11.0`'s registration instants **deliver after their repairs land**, so **a creation's listener is invoked after the creation's writes** (`guide §6`, `:497`–`:513`) — which is precisely *"the event this app never had"*. **The app's silence is therefore no longer a missing capability but a missing entry**: it passes `listeners: []` at both constructions, and **that empty array is the defect** (`D-219`).
- **THE RULE THE DESIGN RESTS ON, STATED AS THE GUIDE STATES IT**: at a registration instant the delivered set is **the adopted listeners whose watched address EXISTS**, *"fire all ones with an existing address after constraints pass"* (`guide §6`, `:536`, the architect's verbatim, `D-123` item 2). **So the creation does not deliver only what a repair wrote** — it delivers to every adopted listener whose ADDRESS stands, and a second boot over an existing profile delivers too, because the `file` families stand and the `mem` families are re-minted by their own repairs. **This single sentence is what makes the fix work on a SECOND boot**, and it is the ground §4 corrects a `D-218` clause on (`D-219`, `D-218`).
- **AND THE APP MAY NOT REGISTER AFTER THE CONSTRUCTION INSTEAD, WHICH IS WHY THIS IS A MOVE AND NOT A TUNING.** `viewSurface.attach()` → `record.subscribe(...)` → `listen`+`register`, and `tabsSurface.attach()` likewise, run **after** `createProfileStore` returns (`src/main/main.ts`, the two `attach()` calls). Leaving them **and** adding `cfg.listeners` entries would be **two registrations for one address — the copy `D-129` forbids**, and a crossing with two listeners delivers twice per write. **So each family's registration MOVES to the construction and the post-construction act is deleted** (`D-219`, `D-93`).

## 3. The listener's body and its queue — because the creation fires BEFORE the window exists

- **THE ORDER IS THE HAZARD, AND IT IS `main()`'S OWN**: the store is constructed as the boot's second act and `const win = new BrowserWindow({…})` is the eleventh (`D-19`'s sequence, `src/main/main.ts`'s own comment). **So a creation listener fires when `BrowserWindow.getAllWindows()` is EMPTY** — the same fact `D-218` measured on the fan-out — and **a push sent there has no destination and is DROPPED**. **`D-219` names the consequence and this section designs it**: *"the listener must QUEUE the addresses and `attachWindow` must flush them"* (`D-219` (i)).
- **THE QUEUE IS ONE PER CROSSING, IT IS `main()`'S, AND IT HOLDS ADDRESSES.** `createDeliveryQueue(push)` answers three members — `deliver(address)`, `arm()`, `held` (§6, `signatures.md` §5). `push` is the crossing's existing fan-out loop, **hoisted above the store's construction and unchanged in body**: *"every window is told, and the push carries no value"*.
  - **`deliver(address)` is the listener's whole act, and it is TOTAL**: armed → `push()`; not armed → **hold the address verbatim**. It **runs no store verb, reads no family and formats nothing** — a listener body's write would open a commit of its own inside the one that fired it (`guide §6.5` item 1), and the only correct answer to a delivery is the consumer's re-read (`D-92`, `D-137`).
  - **`arm()` is the flush and it is called at the ONE instant a push has a destination** (§4). It sets the flag and emits **ONE push for the crossing**, then clears `held`.
  - **`held` is a READING and never a destination**: an insertion-ordered list of the addresses the construction delivered while nothing could hear them. A battery may read it; nothing else may.
- **WHY THE QUEUE IS NOT A CACHE OF VALUES, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS ONE TYPE.** A queue of readings would be a **second authority beside the record** — `D-129`'s class, and the thing `fill()`'s boot read was accused of being (`D-217`). **What is held is the delivery's OWN ARGUMENT: an address, which is all a push may ever carry** (`D-92`, `guide §6.4`). **The flush therefore translates nothing**: it re-emits the crossing's one signal, exactly as a write's delivery does, and the renderer re-reads — *"the push is a SIGNAL and the read is the consumer's"* (`src/main/main.ts`'s own comment on the three pushes).
- **TWO CREATIONS RUNNING IS TWO QUEUES, NOT ONE FULLER ONE.** Each crossing has its own store construction path and therefore its own queue: the central store's `createProfileStore` (panes/zones/theme/drag/tabs) and `operator-settings`' instance, whose subscription is that module's and whose construction is **not this page's** (§7). **Two creations can only mean two crossings, and the queues cannot collide** — and where one crossing ever did deliver twice into one queue, **the arm still emits ONE push**, so dedup is structural rather than counted.
- **A WINDOW THAT ATTACHES TWICE IS THE CASE THAT DECIDES THE ARM'S SHAPE.** `ipcMain.on(IPC_READY, …)` fires once per realm: a fresh window, and again after a **reload** (`did-finish-load` → `handleReset`, `RendererBackend.attachWindow`'s own rearm). **A reloaded realm has read NOTHING**, so a queue that flushed only when it had something held would leave a reloaded app unpainted until the next record change — **a new silence of exactly the class this pass exists to remove**. **So `arm()` is unconditional**: every arrival owes that realm one delivery per crossing, and `held`'s content is the *evidence* that the creation delivered, not the trigger. **The cleared list keeps the arm from growing with the app's age**; the flag is idempotent within a realm and reset by the next arrival, which is what makes a reload the same act as a first boot (`D-216`, `D-217`, `src/main/mcp-server.ts`'s `attachWindow`).

## 4. What the flush hangs off: the realm's arrival, NOT `attachWindow` — a contradiction of `D-219`'s letter, stated as one

- **`D-219` SAYS `attachWindow` MUST FLUSH THEM, AND IN THIS TREE `attachWindow` CANNOT**: `backend.attachWindow(win)` runs **immediately after `new BrowserWindow` and BEFORE `win.loadFile(rendererHtml)`** (`src/main/main.ts`), so at that instant the renderer process does not exist, `webContents.send` is discarded, and **a flush there would empty the queue into nothing — `D-218`'s defect re-created by hand**. **The member `D-219` names is `mcp-surface`'s** (`src/main/mcp-server.ts`'s `attachWindow`, which binds `did-finish-load`/`closed`/`destroyed` and nothing about deliveries), and it is **not this module's** to give a second subject (`D-3`).
- **THE INSTANT THE DESIGN TAKES IS THE APP'S OWN ARRIVAL SIGNAL, `IPC_READY`** — `bridge.ready()` in the renderer's `main()`, `ipcMain.on(IPC_READY, …)` in `main()`, already the boot's readiness edge (`D-19`'s clause, `D-112`). **The arm lands immediately after `backend.markReady()`**, one queue at a time, in the crossings' registration order (tabs, then view).
- **WHY THAT INSTANT IS EARLY ENOUGH, AND WHY THE ARGUMENT IS AN ORDERING ONE AND NOT A HOPE**: the renderer calls `bridge.ready()` — an `ipcRenderer.send`, not an `invoke` — and **then registers every delivery handler synchronously in the same turn** (`startTabsClientInstrument`, `startViewShape`, `startAppStateShape`, `startSecurityShape` all call `client.subscribe(...)` before their first `await`). Main's push is a *later macrotask* on the renderer's loop, so it cannot be dispatched before the turn that sent the readiness finishes. **The delivery therefore finds every `ipcRenderer.on` standing.** **This is an argument and not a measurement, and it is the one thing in this page a battery must prove** (§8 `AL-13`, `D-40`).
- **AND THE ALTERNATIVES ARE NAMED SO THEY ARE NOT REDISCOVERED**: a **timer** or a **retry** after the load is `D-82` clause 4's own prohibition and `D-216`'s four off-paradigm kinds; a **new IPC channel** announcing "subscriptions armed" is a bridge member added to prove a boot, which `D-18` refuses and `D-123` would have to spell in a file the MCP-reachable stack imports; and **awaiting `win.loadFile` and pushing there** is `attachWindow`'s failure one instant later — the renderer's `main()` has not run.

## 6. The signatures this page declares (exact text: `signatures.md` §5)

```ts
interface DeliveryQueue {
  /** THE LISTENER'S WHOLE ACT: the delivered ADDRESS — never a value, never a read (D-92). Total. */
  deliver(address: readonly string[]): void
  /** THE REALM HAS ARRIVED: one push for this crossing, then the held addresses are dropped. Idempotent. */
  arm(): void
  /** WHAT WAS DELIVERED WITH NO DESTINATION — a reading for a battery, never a destination. */
  readonly held: readonly (readonly string[])[]
}
function createDeliveryQueue(push: () => void): DeliveryQueue
```

- **`deliver` IS THE SAME FUNCTION AT EVERY INSTANT**, which is what lets the crossings' entry builders be pure and the store's own construction take them before any surface exists (§2, §3). **It never throws**: a listener body that throws at a registration instant refuses the act at rank `21` `ListenerBodyThrew` (`guide §6`, `:513`–`:516`, `D-124` item 2), which at the creation means `storeCreate` refuses and the app takes `D-80`'s fatal arm — **so the entry's containment and the queue's totality are one guardrail, not two** (§7 `G-2`). **`arm` is NOT `attach` AND IS NOT ON THE SURFACE**: the surfaces' `attach`/`detach` are deleted (§2 `A-4`), and `arm` is `main()`'s, called from the readiness handler only.

## 9. `fill()`'s fate: CONFIRMED, with one of its three reasons CORRECTED and its boot site's meaning NARROWED
