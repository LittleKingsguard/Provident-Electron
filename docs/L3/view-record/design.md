## 1. The structure — five files, one owner each

The record is the crossing's second declaration set over the APP'S ONE STORE: it constructs nothing, opens no file and names the package nowhere (D-87, D-91, D-97, D-142).

| File | Holds | Why it is separate |
|---|---|---|
| `src/main/view-record/index.ts` | the record over the app's one store: **four** address constants (three landed, `VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS` L4's), **three** batched declarations, **seven** routes (`read`, `commitPane`, `commitZones`, `commitTheme`, `commitDrag`, `clearDrag`, `subscribe`) — no instance, no file (D-87, D-91, D-97, D-132) — and the ONLY site in the tree that calls the appearance mechanism | every write is one whole family at its own root, so the surface holds no store verb and `main.ts` holds no address (D-142) |
| `src/main/view-surface/index.ts` | the surface: the one reference to the record, the one subscription over every root the record declares, and the shape arms — `commitDrag(id: unknown, geometry: unknown)` takes `unknown` for `commitPane`'s reason | the record is held once at boot, and this module is the only file a channel handler reaches it through (D-87) |
| `src/shared/view-channels.ts` | the channel names single-sourced: **seven** after the two drag constants — `IPC_VIEW_READ`, `IPC_VIEW_PANE_COMMIT`, `IPC_VIEW_ZONES_COMMIT`, `IPC_VIEW_THEME_COMMIT`, the two drag channels and `IPC_VIEW_CHANGED`; **six carry a request**, and **its header re-counts** in the same hunk (D-87, D-132, D-41) | a name spelled in two files is a rename that half-lands |
| `src/shared/view-shape.ts` | the three families' vocabulary, the measured pane defaults, the appearance placeholder and the pure per-pane/per-zone derivations — ONE spelling for both realms, and **no drag shape**: the reading is `{ size }` under a pane id and needs no type of its own (D-88, D-91, D-132) | two realm-local copies would be two answers to "what is a zone" |
| `src/renderer/view-client.ts` | the shape the RENDERER reads, filled by deliveries, read synchronously, written through the routes — and the drag's two routes carrying the receipt word `'drag'` (D-89, D-137, D-132) | it holds no address space, no persistence, no constraint and no instance |

## 2. The four addresses — and the one that is DECLARED AND UNPAIRED

Each family is ONE whole value at its own root, and `ViewTier` is the **four** tier constants' union, so no read, write or repair spells a tier literal (D-87, D-91, D-97, D-98, D-105, D-132).

| Address | Tier constant | Declaration id | Holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `['panes']` | `PANES_TIER` = `file` | `VIEW_PANES_MUST_STAND_ID` | one member per pane id, each `{ size, bounds }` (D-87) |
| `['zones']` | `ZONES_TIER` = `mem` | `VIEW_ZONES_MUST_STAND_ID` | one member per zone id, four independently optional members (D-91) |
| `['theme']` | `THEME_TIER` = `file` | `VIEW_THEME_MUST_STAND_ID` | one whole record `{ token?, attribute?, declaration }` (D-97, D-98) |
| `['drag']` | `DRAG_TIER` = `temp` | **NONE — there is no pair at this root** | the gesture's in-process reading: one member per pane id, each `{ size }`, present while a drag is in process and **ABSENT once it ends** (D-132) |

**THE FOURTH ENTRY IS DECLARED AND UNPAIRED, AND THE STRUCTURAL REASON IS TWO CLAUSES.** No invariant holds the family — a `temp` reading is a gesture's transient fact, and nothing needs it to exist — **and a repair's subject would be "no drag has started"**, so a pair here could only mint a placeholder for data whose ABSENCE is the product (D-132, D-101). So the root carries an address, a tier constant and a `viewDeclaredRoots()` entry, and **`viewConstraintDeclarations` gains no row**: the guard itself is untroubled, because `declaredTierLedger` keys on the roots it is HANDED rather than on the batch, so a `file` or `mem` write at `['drag']` is refused `declared-tier` ahead of any store verb. **What the entry buys is therefore the family's ONE judgement, and it is the tier's** — and it is the whole of what a `temp` family needs. The family is also the only one with **no composer and no boot datum**: `['drag']` is a root holding one member and no sibling, so nothing is left to preserve and the first move writes the root itself (D-135, D-136, D-138).

**AND THE ENTRY IS NOT LANDABLE ALONE, BECAUSE THE SET ROW IS THE ROOTS' OWNER (D-43, D-101, D-40).** `tests/integration/central-data/declared-root.test.ts`'s `D-1` asserts the published roots **equal** the declared batch's roots, that the publication is one pair per root, and each root's tier — all gating and unmarked — so a root that is declared and carries no pair moves that set and reds that floor the moment it lands. **The entry therefore lands WITH that row's re-grain, which is `central-data`'s own L2 test pass (`E-6`, L1 §7) and not this module's**: the row is re-grained to state that the published set COVERS the declared one and that the one root without a pair is declared lawfully. **This page restates neither the published set nor how many roots stand ACROSS the two crossings** — that count is that row's to state, and it is stated nowhere else.

## 3. The same-edit rules, stated as arms of one rule

- **The entry and its family are ONE HUNK** — and for the drag root the hunk is **the address, the tier constant and the entry together, with no declaration** (D-101, D-132): an entry outliving its family hands the guard a tier for a root that is gone, and an address with no entry leaves the family shadowable at another tier. **AND THAT HUNK OWES ITS CENSUS A COMPANION EDIT**: `central-data`'s `D-1` is the set's owner (D-43), so the drag entry and that row's re-grain are one pass — an entry landed alone reds a gating floor (§2, `E-6`).
- **`subscribe`'s array gains `VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS` IN THE SAME HUNK as the constants** (D-137, `R-2`): `createViewRecord`'s `subscribe` ranges over `[VIEW_PANES_ADDRESS, VIEW_ZONES_ADDRESS, VIEW_THEME_ADDRESS]` today, and a `temp` root the array omits is a write the renderer never hears — the one place this can fail silently. **THE LISTENER IS TIER-BLIND**: `standing.listen(handle, [address])` binds an ADDRESS and no tier — the store's own `listen` takes addresses and never a tier — so an outside `provident.data.write({path:['drag'], tier:'temp'})` fires the same delivery the drag's own commit does, and the consumer's only correct answer is a re-read (`P-3`, `D-137`).
- **The seven sites are ONE EDIT** (D-14, D-112): the channel constants, the record's two routes, the surface's two arms, the two `ipcMain.handle` sites, **both lockstep halves** and the client's two methods. A client method whose channel has no handler writes nowhere, and a bridge member landed at one half only answers a route the other cannot reach — **which is this tree's state at this head**: `src/renderer/secure-panels.ts`'s `view?` block carries `commitDrag`/`clearDrag` (`preload.ts`'s `view` does not), so the preload half and the two handler sites are `E-3`'s and land with `E-4`'s already-written text unchanged (D-112).
- **The drag root's hunk is smaller than a family's usual pieces and is still one hunk**: address + tier + entry (no id, no body, no repair, no batch row), the two routes, the array entry.

## 4. `read()`'s answer, as landed — three families, and no drag member

`read()` answers `{ status: 'committed'; panes; zones; theme; source: { panes; zones; theme } }` — `reading()` in `createViewRecord` is the body — and **no `drag` member stands on either side of the wire** on purpose: the temp reading is read where the tiers are read, `provident.data.read({path: ['drag']})`, whose node carries its own `_tier` (D-133, D-144). So a consumer asks the readout, `read()` stays total with no refused arm and no `repaired` member, `paneGeometryIn`/`zoneIn` stay the pure derivations, and **no `dragOf` exists** to be a second answer (D-84, D-70, D-105, L2 §3).

## 5. The three declarations — and the value nothing judges

`panes-must-stand`, `zones-must-stand` and `theme-must-stand`, gathered by `viewConstraintDeclarations(getStore)` for the boot's ONE batch; each body judges `context.value` and none is wrapped by a null-store guard, because the boot binds its holder only after `createProfileStore` returns (D-65, D-87, D-91, D-97). Existence is repaired with a placeholder per family (`repairPanes`, `repairZones`, `repairTheme`, through the one path `writeWholeValue`); panes and zones answer a SECOND arm that is a refusal with no repair (`panesAdmitAt`/`zonesAdmitAt` at the node the write is at, `writeInstant` abstaining at a registration instant); and `theme-must-stand`'s staleness arm is its family's whole reason (`derivedTheme`, `sameThemeValue`, the tree's only `resolveTheme`/`applyThemeDeclaration` call) (D-105, D-97, D-98).

**NOTHING JUDGES A VALUE AT `temp`** (D-105, D-132): no pair stands there, so the two routes' shape arms are the whole gate and the store's own boundary is the rest — and the drag routes **mint no token of their own**: their two faults ARE the panes' two, so `malformed-pane` and `malformed-geometry` answer them and no third token arrives.

## 6. What is structurally untouched

- **The appearance family's constraint-repair product and its `file` tier**, the zones family as built (address, `mem`, pair, `commitZones` over `composeZones`), and the panes family (`panes-must-stand`, `paneGeometryIn`'s default arm, `commitPane` as the release's unchanged `file` commit) (D-97, D-98, D-91, D-87, D-132).
- **The guard, the batch and every composer**: `src/main/data/surface.ts` reads the roots it is handed, `src/main/main.ts`'s batch gains no declaration, `composePanes` keeps its merge for the `file` commit alone, and the drag root needs no composer at all (D-101, D-135, D-138).
- **The creation-instant pair per family**, the boot's ONE `createProfileStore` batch, the `view-record:` handle prefix no constraint id repeats, and the delivery that carries a signal and no value — now over every root this module declares (D-71, D-87, D-137).
- **`isBoundary`** (the zone family's `slot`), `nodeOf`/`writeInstant` (the two value arms) and `writeWholeValue`/`repairAnswer` (the three repairs' one write path) all keep their readers.

## 7. Pseudocode — every body L4 changes

- **The address block** (`src/main/view-record/index.ts`, beside `VIEW_PANES_ADDRESS`/`PANES_TIER`): `export const VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS: readonly string[] = ['drag']` and `const DRAG_TIER = 'temp'`; then `type ViewTier = typeof PANES_TIER | typeof ZONES_TIER | typeof THEME_TIER | typeof DRAG_TIER`. **No id constant, no batch row, no repair** — and `D-135`'s composer-comment correction rides this pass, since it is the pass that opens the file.
- **`viewDeclaredRoots()`**: append `{ address: VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS, tier: DRAG_TIER }` to the three landed pairs; `ViewDeclaredRoot.tier` is already `ViewTier`, so the entry type-checks only once the union gains the word.
- **`ViewRecord`'s two declarations**: `commitDrag(id: string, geometry: unknown): ViewRecordAnswer` and `clearDrag(): ViewRecordAnswer`, beside the three commits and `subscribe`.
- **`commitDrag(id, geometry)`**: if `id` is not a non-empty string → refused `PANE_SHAPE_REASON`, `address: null`; if `geometry` is not a plain record → the same arm; then if its `size` is not a finite non-negative number → refused `PANE_GEOMETRY_REASON` with `address: [VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS[0] as string, id]`; then `try { standing.write(VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS, DRAG_TIER, { [id]: { size } }); return { status: 'committed' } } catch (caught) { …refusalOfThrown(caught)… }` — the refusal carrying the member's address. **THE `size` IS REQUIRED HERE AND THAT IS THE ONE PLACE THIS ARM IS STRICTER THAN `commitPane`'S**: a `commitPane` member may be supplied alone because the missing one is merged from what stands, while the drag family's member IS the placement — and `undefined` is not a value the crossing carries, which is the module's own `isBoundary` predicate read at the same file (D-87, D-91). **ONE WHOLE VALUE THE CALLER FORMATTED AND NO COMPOSER**: nothing stands at a sibling to preserve, so there is no merge to perform (D-136, D-135, D-138).
- **`clearDrag()`**: `try { standing.delete(VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS, DRAG_TIER); return { status: 'committed' } } catch (caught) { …refusalOfThrown(caught)… }` with `address: [VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS[0] as string]` — **a DELETE and never a placeholder**, so the address answers `absent` after the gesture (ASM-2, L2 §2). **AND IT IS `committed` WHETHER OR NOT THE FAMILY STOOD**, which needs no arm of its own: a delete that removes nothing is the store's own success (`tiers: []`), the fact the tab record's `remove` relies on at its own absent-tab branch (`src/main/tabs-record/index.ts`) — read there rather than re-derived here (D-53).
- **`subscribe`'s array**: the loop's literal gains a fourth address — `[VIEW_PANES_ADDRESS, VIEW_ZONES_ADDRESS, VIEW_THEME_ADDRESS, VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS]` — with the per-address `listen`+`register`, the containment of the caller's throw and the idempotent withdrawal unchanged (D-71, D-137).
- **The surface**: `ViewSurface` gains `commitDrag(id: unknown, geometry: unknown)` and `clearDrag()`, whose bodies are the shape arms (non-string id, non-plain geometry → `PANE_SHAPE_REASON`/`PANE_SHAPE_MESSAGE`, `address: null`) and then `record.commitDrag(id, geometry)` / `record.clearDrag()`; `attach`'s one subscription covers the fourth root by construction.
- **The channels**: `IPC_VIEW_DRAG_COMMIT = 'provident:view:drag-commit'` and `IPC_VIEW_DRAG_CLEAR = 'provident:view:drag-clear'`, and the file's header re-counts to seven names, six carrying a request.
- **The client, and it is SIX spellings rather than one** (`src/renderer/view-client.ts`): `ViewClientBridge` gains `commitDrag(id: string, geometry: unknown): Promise<unknown>` and `clearDrag(): Promise<unknown>`; **`ViewClient` declares the same two members**, or the factory's returned literal is an excess-property error; `routeOf`'s name union gains `'commitDrag' | 'clearDrag'`; `send`'s route position gains `'drag'`; **`outcomeOf`'s own `route` parameter gains it too**, or `send`'s call is `TS2345`; and `ViewWriteOutcome.route` gains it so a receipt can say which family it belongs to. The bodies are `commitDrag` → `send('drag', routeOf(member, 'commitDrag'), [id, geometry])` and `clearDrag` → `send('drag', routeOf(member, 'clearDrag'), [])` — **no argument on the clear**, the `null` arm staying unspent (D-140).
- **The boot's two handlers are `app-shell`'s `E-3` and the half beside them is `secure-panels`' `E-4`, neither this module's**: this page states only that the preload's two members are the SAME TEXT as `src/renderer/secure-panels.ts:125-126` already carries, that the two `ipcMain.handle` sites sit after the read's, and that the surface's options, store and push do not move (D-112, D-14, D-41).

## 8. Guardrails for L4

- **No constraint pair at `['drag']`** — no id, no body, no repair, no batch row; an entry without a pair is the whole of what a `temp` family needs (D-132, D-101).
- **No placeholder value anywhere at the root**: the clear is a DELETE, and a written empty family would leave a `temp` node standing and make "gone" weaker than row `5`'s word (ASM-2).
- **No member on `read()`** and no `dragOf`, no new `data.*` verb and no new MCP tool: the ghost asks the delivery (D-133, D-144, D-18).
- **No secure-store route**: the family joins the NORMAL crossing beside `['panes']`, and the secure store gains no address, declaration, route or member (D-132).
- **Nothing judges a `temp` value** — no value arm, no coercion, no repair; the store's boundary is the rest (D-105).
- **No composer, no timer, no poll, no write on a read**, and four whole-family `.write(VIEW_…` sites plus one `.delete(VIEW_DRAG_ADDRESS, DRAG_TIER` — the census `crossing.test.ts` clause 4 states as `4` and `zone-family.test.ts`'s `Z-6` re-reads (D-82 clause 4, D-41).
- **No other row's file is this module's**: `src/main/main.ts`, `src/main/preload.ts` and `src/renderer/renderer.ts` are `E-3`/`E-8`'s, and **`src/renderer/secure-panels.ts` already carries the drag pair at this head** — so its half is text to MATCH and never text to write, and the preload's two members join it as the same text in one edit (`E-4`, D-112, L1 §7, D-3).
