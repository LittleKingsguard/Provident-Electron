**⟶ `DEF-AV` — FILED `2026-10-29` AT `1.6.1`: A RAISE'S RECEIPT IS PUBLISHED BEFORE ITS DELIVERY'S VERDICT AND IS NEVER RETRACTED. IT IS THE `DEF-AU` ASYMMETRY READ FROM THE OTHER SIDE, AND IT IS THE ONE BEHAVIOURAL FINDING LEFT IN THIS CATALOGUE.** **`observed`; one script, two arms identical but for one pair (`q2-behavioural.txt`).** **THE CONSTRUCTION: `write(['a'],'mem',5)`; `write(['a'],'mem',7)` captured as `w2`; a pair at `['a']` refusing the value `5`; a listener `L` at `['b']` whose body raises `s.rollback(w2.op.opId)` and records its own answer; then the caller writes `['b']=1`, which is what delivers `L`.**
- **arm `refuse=true`** — **the LISTENER is told `RETURNED {"opId":"op-2","undoneOps":["op-2"],"restoredTo":{"present":true,"value":5}}`**; **the CALLER is told `THREW ConstraintPassRefused` with `address: ["a"]`**; **and the STORE says `read a = 7`** — **the raise was UNDONE after its receipt said it had restored `5`.** `read b = 1`.
- **arm `refuse=false`** — the same receipt, **and the store agrees** (`read a = 5`), the caller `RETURNED`. **So the receipt is truthful exactly when its delivery survives, and false when it does not.**

**WHY THIS IS BEHAVIOURAL AND NOT DOCUMENTARY — THE SHIPPED GUIDE MAKES TWO PRESENT-TENSE CLAIMS THE STORE CONTRADICTS.** `README.guide.md:434-437` declares ***"`undoneOps` is the LIST of the ids the reversal actually undid — a report of reversals PERFORMED"*** and ***"`restoredTo` is the base state the addressed subtree NOW STANDS AT, `{ present, value }`"***. **In the `refuse=true` arm `undoneOps` lists `op-2`, whose reversal did not survive, and `restoredTo` says the subtree stands at `5` while it stands at `7`.** **Nothing in the guide declares the receipt provisional, retractable or superseded** (searched: `provisional`, `may be undone`, `is not final`, `receipt` — one unrelated hit at `:1803`). **A listener that logs `receipt.restoredTo` records a state that never existed, and the party misled is the one that PERFORMED the act — the mirror of `DEF-AU`, where the party misled was the one that REQUESTED the delivery.** **Demand, in the package's own terms, any one of: publish the receipt only after the delivery's verdict; retract it through the channel that carries the refusal; or declare the receipt provisional and stop saying "now stands at".** **PUBLISHED-REACHABLE: every listener that reverses anything, which is the mechanism `§5.8` recommends.** **UPSTREAM-owned; NOT PATCHED HERE.**

**⟶ `DEF-AM` — CLOSED AT `1.6.1` BY RE-NAMING AND BY BECOMING A PLAIN VALUE.** The `1.5.0` row measured a `addresses` member that stayed at `length 1` for one, three and twenty-five pairs, was non-enumerable, and was lost by spread, `Object.assign`, `JSON.stringify` and `structuredClone`. **At `1.6.1` the triggered record's own members are `["handle","address"]` — `handle` `enumerable=true` and `address` `enumerable=true`, both plain values, `address` carrying the ONE matched address — and both survive spread, `JSON.stringify` and `structuredClone`.** **A probe of ours printed a lossy spread reading first; run in isolation the same record spreads cleanly, so that reading was OUR instrument and is recorded as such rather than filed.**
