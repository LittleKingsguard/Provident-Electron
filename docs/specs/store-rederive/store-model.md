### 2.2 The four tiers, the persistence order, and the monotonic invariant

1. **FOUR TIERS — `secure`, `file`, `mem`, `temp`** (`A C5`: *"four tiers (secure, file, mem, temp)"*). Terms: `4 = 1 (secure, outside every order) + 3 (the ranked tiers: file · mem · temp)` ✓. **The generic surface's tier domain is the three ranked tokens; `secure` is not a legal argument anywhere on the generic surface** (`docs/specs/store-rederive/seams-and-lifecycle.md §2.9` item 1; `C6`'s separate-functions leg). `(A C5 · A C6 · CONTRACT + CENSUS)`
2. **THE PERSISTENCE ORDER (`R9`): `file` > `mem` > `temp`, with `secure` OUTSIDE every ordering.** Terms: `3` ranked tokens with `3` ordered pairs (`file>mem`, `file>temp`, `mem>temp`) ✓. **The order is the meaning of every "lower-persistence" and "lowest-persistence" phrase in this contract** (`C19`'s purge, `C20`'s lower-tier write, `R9`'s read): **"lower persistence" = strictly less persistent = the tier that appears to the RIGHT of the other in `file > mem > temp`.** `(A R9 · A C19 · A C20 · CONTRACT)`
3. **THE MONOTONIC INVARIANT, CARRIED AS A CLAUSE OF ITS OWN, NOT AS A PREMISE (`R9` item 3, which the architect required the successor to carry):** **A NODE MAY NOT BE MORE PERSISTENT THAN ITS PARENT.** For every parent-side anchor's link, `rank(child node's tier) ≤ rank(parent node's tier)`, where `rank(file)=3 > rank(mem)=2 > rank(temp)=1`. **A write whose tier out-ranks the parent instance's tier is REFUSED** (`'persistence-inversion'`, step `F-TIER`). **The root of a tree has no parent and the invariant is vacuously satisfied there** (`C14`). **Terms: the `9 = 3 × 3` tier pairs, of which `6` are legal and `3` are refused** (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.5`). `(A R9 · CONTRACT + CENSUS)`
4. **`secure` IS A SEPARATE GRAPH, SAVED TO A SEPARATE FILE, AND IT FOLLOWS THE SAME CONSTRAINTS AS `file` FOR ALL OTHER PURPOSES** (`A C6`) — so the commit duty (`C7`/`R3`/`R8`), the tombstone lifecycle (`R12`), the mint rules (`C3`/`C11`) and the event surface (`C22`–`C24`) apply inside it, with the two differences `C6` names (its own functions; its own file) plus the gate (`docs/specs/store-rederive/seams-and-lifecycle.md §2.9`). **Within the `secure` graph the tier is NOT a member of the node and NOT a parameter — `C11`'s own words: *"secure is determined by being in an isolated graph"*** (`A C11`). `(A C6 · A C11 · CONTRACT)`

### 2.3 The address space and the walk

1. **AN ADDRESS IS A NON-EMPTY SEGMENT LIST: `Address = ReadonlyArray<string>`, every segment a non-empty string.** **The store declares NO dotted-string parser and NO tier segment** (`docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-10`, `DD-20`; `R6`; `C16`'s walk is segment-by-segment: *"Register is checked for node named 'window', node is checked for anchor at property 'tabs', associated link is checked for node named, 'landingPage'"*). **A segment is ANY non-empty string — the store declares NO segment alphabet, NO escaping and NO reserved spelling** (`R2` forbids a whitelist, and `R1` forbids a name set that serves an example; the `S-1`-row's carried half — segment domain and escaping — is thereby DISCHARGED as an empty domain restriction). `(A C16 · A R1 · A R2 · CONTRACT + DECLARED-DEFAULT)`
   - **OUTSIDE the address domain** (all refused `'malformed-address'`, step `A-ADDRESS`, `segment: null`): a non-array · an empty array · a non-string segment · an empty-string segment. **A dotted string is OUTSIDE the domain** (it is not an array) — the presentation is the caller's. Terms: `4` malformed arms (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.7`). `(T · CONTRACT)`
2. **THE WALK RUNS FROM THE REGISTER** (`A C16`; `A C14`: *"Register contains root nodes of trees"*). The walk's arms, in order, with their step ids and their failures:
   - **`A-ADDRESS`** — parse and validate the address (`item 1`); failure ⇒ `'malformed-address'`.
   - **`B-ROOT`** — the register's entry named `address[0]` must exist; otherwise **`'unknown-root'`** (the store declares **NO declaration input, NO reserved names and NO cold-root concept**: a root comes into being **only** by a write at a 1-segment address, `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.5` item 3). `(A C14 · A C18 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`
   - **`C-ANCHOR`** — at the current node, the **`parent`-type** anchor whose `key === address[i]` must exist (a `child`-type anchor is **NEVER** a walk step: **a row that lets the walk follow a `child`-type anchor FAILS**, `§2.3` item 4).
   - **`D-LINK`** — that anchor's link list must hold at least one link (`C12`: *"anchors are the interface between nodes and links"* — the anchor carries the links that hang children under its property).
   - **`E-CHILD`** — **among the anchor's links, the walk must select a link whose child-side node exists**; the child node is the node the prefix `address[0..i]` names.
   - **`F-TIER`** — the selection among the anchor's links is the READ's rule (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.8` item 2) or the WRITE's rule (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.5` item 4); its failure is `'tier-not-held'` (read) or the add arm (write).
   - **THE TERMINAL ARM, AND WHY THE WALK IS THE WHOLE ADDRESS RULE:** the walk consumes segment `i ≥ 2` by following `C-ANCHOR` → `D-LINK` → `E-CHILD`, so **the node named by the prefix `address[0..i]` is the child-side node reached at `i`**. With `n = address.length`: `i = n` reaches **the addressed node**. **A failure at an INTERMEDIATE segment (`i < n`) is `'unreachable'`** (`A C17`: *"If an address is unreachable (ex. no anchor at 'window.tabs'), return verbose error"*), **and NOTHING IS MINTED** (`docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-11`: an intermediate node would carry no literals, and `C11` says *"nodes store the literals they are written with"*). Terms: `4` walk steps `+ 1` parse step `= 5` steps (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.3`). `(A C16 · A C17 · A C11 · CONTRACT + DECLARED-DEFAULT)`
   - **⟶ `M-PROP`, APPENDED 2026-10-08 (`R16`/`R17`, THE ARCHITECT): THE FINAL SEGMENT'S RESOLUTION STEP.** **When the walk reaches the IMMEDIATE PARENT `P` of the addressed address — `P = address[0 .. n-1]`, the node the walk reaches at `i = n − 1` (`n = 1` ⇒ `P` is the REGISTER, whose entry is `C14`'s link-without-a-parent) — the store takes ONE further decision, at step `M-PROP` (`§2.1`'s appended step id), and it is the arm-selector of `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.5` item 7 and `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.8` item 7: WHAT DOES THE FINAL SEGMENT NAME AT `P`?** **THE FOUR ANSWERS, AND NOTHING ELSE (`§2.1`'s `PropertyResolution`, printed there and never returned to a caller):** **(i) A NODE LOCATION** — `P`'s node carries a `parent`-type anchor at the final segment whose link holds a child-side node: *"an address that resolves to being contained in an existing link"* (`R16`(d) verbatim). **(ii) A LITERAL PROPERTY** — `P`'s node carries the final segment **in its `literals`** (`C11`), and the segment is not `_value` (`item 5`). **(`_value` standing as a literal is the FOURTH state — a TRANSPARENT NODE — and it resolves as a NODE LOCATION whose node is transparent: the node IS addressable, and only its READ is transparent, `item 5`; a TRANSPARENT NODE therefore carries exactly one literal and no anchor (`R17`(c)).)** **(iii) AN ABSENT PROPERTY** — `P` resolves and its node carries **NEITHER** a literal at that segment **NOR** a `parent` anchor at it: `C18`'s case, *"a node that could exist, but doesn't"* — and it is a **PROPERTY ADDRESS** in `R16`(d)'s sense (there is no link there to contain anything, so the segment names a property of `P`). **(iv) NO RESOLUTION** — `P` does not resolve at all (`C17`): **`R16`(a)'s own condition — *"the address's immediate parent resolves to a node"* — is FALSE**, so `R16`(a) does not apply and no arm may mint. **Terms: `4 = 1 (node location) + 1 (literal property) + 1 (absent property) + 1 (no resolution)` ✓ (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.2`).** **THE DISAMBIGUATION IS BY `C11`'s OWN STRUCTURE AND NOT BY A MARKER: a node's members are its `literals` and its `parent` anchors (`§2.4` item 6), so the segment is looked up in the node's literal table FIRST and then among its `parent` anchors' `key`s; `C21`'s duplicate ban is what makes this a function and not a search — the store's own write logic forbids one address-and-tier pair twice (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.8` item 6), so at this step exactly one of the four answers holds.** `(A R16 · A R17 · A C11 · A C16 · A C18 · T · CONTRACT + CENSUS)`
3. **THE REGISTER (`C14`) — ITS ENTRIES AND NOTHING ELSE.** *"Register contains root nodes of trees. It has the functionality of a link, without having a distinct parent."* An entry is `{ name: string, node: Node, constraints: ConstraintRegistration[] }` — **the root name as the entry's child order/key, the root node as its child, and the link-functionality's constraint list; and NO parent reference** (`C14`'s own subtraction). **The register carries no cache, no staleness flag, no reservation, no declaration input and no cap** (`R1`/`R4`; the inventory's `[H]` rows for the landed cache/reservation/two-root-caps are `STALE PENDING`, `L`; `docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §1` item 2's `NEGATIVE`). Terms: `4` asserted parts (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.4`). `(A C14 · R §4.1 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE + CENSUS)`
4. **THE NODE, THE ANCHOR AND THE LINK — THE DECLARED RECORD SHAPES, EVERY MEMBER CITING ITS `Cn`.** `C11`: *"nodes store the literals they are written with and the tier of the store they are in … Any properties that refer to other objects are replaced by `parent` anchors"*; `C12`: *"anchors are the interface between nodes and links. They contain their parent and link references, their type (parent/child) and their child order/key"*; `C13`: *"Links can contain constraint functions"*. `(A C11 · A C12 · A C13 · CONTRACT)`

```
Node   = { tier: Tier,                       // C11 — the tier of the store it is in (ABSENT in the secure graph, C11)
           literals: { [member: string]: StoreValue },   // C11 — the literals it was written with
           anchors: Anchor[] }              // C11/C12 — one parent anchor per object-valued member
Anchor = { parent: NodeRef,                 // C12 — "their parent … reference" (the link's parent-side node)
           links: Link[],                   // C12 — the interface between the node and its links
           type: 'parent' | 'child',        // C12 — "their type (parent/child)"; STORED, docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A DD-4
           key: string,                     // C12 — "child order/key" (1): the SEGMENT: the dict key
           order: number }                  // C12 — "child order/key" (2): the dense ORDINAL: the array index
Link   = { parent: AnchorRef, child: AnchorRef, constraints: ConstraintRegistration[] }  // C13
RegisterEntry = { name: string, node: NodeRef, constraints: ConstraintRegistration[] }   // C14
```

   - **THE `type` MEMBER IS STORED AND IS OBSERVABLE, AND HERE IS THE OBSERVABLE:** a `parent`-type anchor sits on the node that owns the object-valued property; a `child`-type anchor sits on the CHILD node and marks the same link's other side, carrying the SAME `key`/`order`/`parent`/`links`. **Because the two sides carry the same `key`, the walk's `C-ANCHOR` arm MUST filter on `type === 'parent'`** — otherwise a read of `address ⊕ [key]` at the child would follow the child-side anchor back onto its own node or its parent. **A row in which a `child`-type anchor is a walk step FAILS.** `(A C12 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`
   - **⟶ APPENDED BESIDE 2026-10-08 (`R16`(e)/`R17`(c), THE ARCHITECT): THE NODE'S MEMBER TABLE `literals` MAY CARRY THE RESERVED KEY `_value`, AND NOTHING ELSE CHANGES IN THE SHAPE** — `C11`'s *"nodes store the literals they were written with"* is the whole carrier, so **the transparency of `item 5` needs NO new node member, NO flag, NO marker and NO second member table: a TRANSPARENT NODE is the node whose `literals` holds `_value` and NOTHING ELSE, and whose `anchors` is EMPTY** (`R17`(c) verbatim: *"Exclusive — `_value` is alone in its node"*). **Terms: `1` reserved key in the node's `literals` table; the node's member count does not move.** `(A R16 · A R17 · A C11 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`

5. **⟶ THE `_value` RESERVATION AND WHERE THE RULE BITES IN THE ADDRESS GRAMMAR — APPENDED 2026-10-08 (`R16`(e) VERBATIM, `R17`(c) VERBATIM, THE ARCHITECT; `docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-23`).** `R16`(e): *"A node should be able to be made "transparent" on read (ex. write [true, 123, "Foo"] --> link with nodes of values true, 123, "Foo"] --> read [true, 123, "Foo"]) by assigning the literal value to the `_value` property. This is otherwise a reserved keyword."* `R17`(c): *"Exclusive — `_value` is alone in its node"*. **Five clauses follow, and each is a row a TestWriter can drive:**
   - **(a) A TRANSPARENT NODE IS A NODE WHOSE `literals` HOLDS EXACTLY ONE MEMBER, `_value`, WHOSE VALUE IS A LITERAL — and no `anchors` at all.** **It IS ADDRESSABLE: the address it stands at resolves to it, `M-PROP` classifies it as a NODE LOCATION (item 2's fourth state), and a TIER-QUALIFIED read at that address still returns the node — the qualified read reports `tier` and answers the node's transcription (`{_value: <the literal>}`), because a qualified read asks WHICH INSTANCE, not WHAT IT READS AS.** **The TRANSPARENCY is a property of the UNQUALIFIED read alone (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.8` item 7).** `(A R16 · A R17 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`
   - **(b) `_value` IS RESERVED — *"This is otherwise a reserved keyword"* — SO A CALLER'S ORDINARY PROPERTY OF THAT NAME IS NOT AVAILABLE.** **`RESERVED_VALUE_MEMBER` (`docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.1`) is NOT a member a caller writes as ordinary data: a caller who wants the transparency asks for it with the write payload's `transparent` member (`docs/specs/store-rederive/parameter-semantics.md §6` `PAR-14`), and a caller who writes `{value: 'x'}` at a node location gets an ORDINARY literal member named `x`'s member key — never `_value`.** **The reservation is what keeps ONE key from carrying TWO meanings: without it, a node's `_value` literal would be at once a caller's ordinary datum and the store's own transparency marker, and no read could tell the two apart.** **The reservation is NOT an address-grammar reservation: `PAR-1` stands — any non-empty string is a legal SEGMENT, so an address MAY name a segment spelled `_value`; what is reserved is the MEMBER KEY AT A NODE, and the two are different referents.** `(A R16 · A C11 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`
   - **(c) EXCLUSIVITY (`R17`(c)): `_value` IS ALONE IN ITS NODE.** **A transparent node carries EXACTLY ONE literal and NOTHING ELSE.** **A write that would place any SECOND member alongside a `_value` literal — a second literal, or a container-member (which would mint an anchor) — is REFUSED `'value-not-admitted'` (token 9, REUSED — see (e)), and a CONTAINER written to `_value` is REFUSED the same token (`P §12`'s first carriage: *"a write of an object/array to `_value` is refused `'value-not-admitted'`"*).** **WHY THE EXCLUSIVITY IS NOT COSMETIC: an unqualified read of a transparent node answers the `_value` literal ALONE, so ANY second member would be RETAINED IN THE STORE WHILE BEING UNREADABLE THROUGH THE UNQUALIFIED READ — a member no read can reach is a member the contract must not permit.** `(A R17 · A R16 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`
   - **(d) THE TOMBSTONE'S REACH AT A TRANSPARENT NODE.** **A tombstone written at a transparent node deletes it exactly as any other node's tombstone does** (`P §12`'s second carriage; `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.6` items 1–3 unchanged; `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.5` item 7's tombstone arms). **The deleted transparent node is not returned by any read, its address answers `'tombstoned'` unqualified, and a qualified read of the tombstone's own tier answers the tombstone's own absence — all exactly as `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.6` item 2 states for every node.** `(A R17 · A R10 · A R11 · CONTRACT)`
   - **(e) THE NEW TOKEN'S RELATION TO THE `_value` REFUSALS, STATED SO THE UNION IS NOT MIS-READ AS GROWING TWICE: `_value`'s TWO refusals — a container at `_value`, and a second member beside a `_value` literal — are token 9 `'value-not-admitted'`, whose class is exactly `docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.4` item 2's admission set and whose as-filed wording already covers them (`PAR-4`); the APPENDED twenty-first token `'node-location-container'` names the SEPARATE fact of `R17`(a) — a container passed to a NODE LOCATION — which is a misplacement and not an inadmissible value.** **Two facts, two tokens, ONE appended:** the union grows by one and only one member. `(A R17 · A R16 · T · CONTRACT + CENSUS)`

### 2.4 The mint — what a write STORES (`C3`, `C11`, `C18`, `C21`)

1. **`C3` — THE MINTING RULE, VERBATIM.** *"Writes mint new nodes/links/anchors to contain the primitive values contained by the received object, never providing a reference as value."* **Therefore: a write never stores a caller's object; it MINTS.** `(A C3 · CONTRACT)`
2. **THE VALUE-ADMISSION SET — `docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-5`, ONE SET FOR EVERY TIER, PRINTED IN FULL.** A write's `payload.value` is ADMITTED iff it is one of the `6` admitted classes below; otherwise the write is REFUSED `'value-not-admitted'` (step `G-VALUE`) and **nothing is minted and no file is written**. `(T · DECLARED-DEFAULT)`
   - **ADMITTED, `6`:** a `string` · a finite `number` other than `-0` · a `boolean` · `null` · **a plain object** whose prototype is `Object.prototype` or `null`, whose every own member key is a NON-EMPTY string, and whose every own member value is admitted (a **dense data member**; an accessor is refused, `item 3`) · **an array** whose own keys are exactly the dense range `0..n-1` and whose every element is admitted.
   - **REFUSED, `16`:** `undefined` · a function · a `symbol` · a `bigint` · `NaN` · `Infinity`/`-Infinity` · `-0` · a cyclic object or array · a `Map`/`Set`/`WeakMap`/`WeakSet` · a `Date` · any object whose prototype is neither `Object.prototype` nor `null` (**class instances included**) · an array with a HOLE · an array with an own member beyond its dense indices · an own ACCESSOR property (a getter is **not invoked** — it is refused) · an own SYMBOL-keyed member · an own member key that is the EMPTY STRING.
   - **WHY THESE AND NOT OTHERS, ONE LINE EACH (the derivation, so the set is not mistaken for taste):** the `file` tier is a JSON file whose bytes must *"exactly match the data state"* (`C7`) — so a value that cannot round-trip through JSON cannot inhabit the tier as data (`NaN`/`±Infinity`/`-0`/`undefined`/functions/symbols/bigints/accessors/holes/non-plain objects: each either vanishes, changes, or cannot be represented) · a member key must be a legal address SEGMENT, because `C15` requires an address for every read and write and `C16` reaches a child by its anchor's key — an empty key would mint a node **no address can name** · `C8`/`C9`'s pre-commit flow (*write at `temp`, test, then write at `file`*) breaks if a value admitted at `temp` is refused at `file`, hence **one set**. `(A C7 · A C15 · A C16 · A C9 · DECLARED-DEFAULT)`
3. **THE MINT'S RECURSION — `C11`'s object-reference rule, whole depth.** `mint(value, tier)` produces a node and its subtree:
   - **the node** carries `tier` (`C11`; absent in the secure graph) and, per own data member of the received object **in `Object.keys` order**:
     - a **LITERAL**-classed member (string, number, boolean, `null`) ⇒ an entry of the node's `literals`, under the member's own key;
     - a **CONTAINER**-classed member (plain object or array) ⇒ **a `parent` anchor whose `key` is the member's name and whose `order` is its dense position among that node's anchors (from `0`)** — `C11`'s *"Any properties that refer to other objects are replaced by `parent` anchors"* — **plus a link and a child node minted by the SAME rule for that member's value (whole depth, recursively)**. `(A C11 · A C3 · CONTRACT)`
   - **THE DEPTH IS THE WHOLE DEPTH.** `C3` says *"the primitive values contained by the received object"* and `C11` says *"any properties that refer to other objects"* — **there is no depth bound, no level limit and no top-level-only arm**; a nested container at any depth is minted and addressable.
   - **NODES CARRY NO NAME.** `C11` enumerates a node's content as **the literals it was written with and its tier** — so **a node does NOT carry its own name, ref, flag or cache**; its name IS the `key` of the `parent` anchor that reaches it, or, at the root, the register entry's `name` (`C14`). `(A C11 · A C14 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`
4. **THE `C18` EXAMPLE, IN THE ARCHITECT'S OWN TERMS, AS THE MINT'S WORKED ILLUSTRATION (an ILLUSTRATION, never a store member):** *"if it is passed data = `{active: true, contents: {...}}`, the node will contain `active: true` and a parent anchor for a link to the 'contents' object"* — the node at the address holds `literals.active = true` and one `parent` anchor `key: 'contents'`, whose link leads to the child node minted for the contents object, itself addressable as `address ⊕ ['contents']`. `(A C18 · CONTRACT)`
5. **NO VALUE IS EVER A REFERENCE, AND NO MINT IS EVER PARTIAL.** A refused write mints nothing; a minted-but-uncommitted file-tier write is discarded wholesale (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.7` item 2). **A row in which a returned or stored value aliases a caller's object, or a store-internal record, FAILS** (`C2`/`C3`). `(A C2 · A C3 · CONTRACT + NEGATIVE)`

6. **⟶ THE NODE'S CONTENT MODEL — THE THREE MEMBER KINDS, AND HOW EACH IS DISTINGUISHED — APPENDED 2026-10-08 (`R16`/`R17`, THE ARCHITECT; `docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-23`).** **`C11`'s own sentence is the carrier — *"nodes store the literals they were written with … Any properties that refer to other objects are replaced by `parent` anchors"* — and the clarification forces it to be PRINTED, because `R16`(a)–(d) and `R17`(b) each turn on WHICH KIND a member is.** **A node holds EXACTLY THREE KINDS OF MEMBER, and there is no fourth:**
   - **(i) LITERAL MEMBERS** — keyed by the member name, valued by a string, a finite `number` (never `-0`), a `boolean` or `null` (`docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.1`'s `StoreValue`; the `4` literal classes of `docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.4` item 3). **Distinguished by WHERE THEY LIVE: an entry of the node's own `literals` table, with no anchor and no link.** **The distinction IS the node's two tables: `literals` versus `anchors` (`docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.3` item 4's record shape, `C11`'s two clauses) — so the store never inspects a value to classify a member, it asks WHICH TABLE the member's key stands in.** Terms: `4` literal classes ✓ (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.2`).
   - **(ii) ANCHOR MEMBERS** — **a `parent`-type anchor whose `key` is the member's name, whose link leads to a child node (`C11`'s object-reference rule), i.e. a member whose value is a container.** **Distinguished by: a `parent` anchor at that key, and a non-empty `links` list (`D-LINK`), and a child-side node (`E-CHILD`).** **`C18`'s worked illustration is exactly this kind: `{active: true, contents: {...}}` ⇒ `literals.active = true` + one `parent` anchor `key:'contents'` (`docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.4` item 4).** **Terms: `1` anchor member kind (the container-valued property), driven in `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.5` item 7 and `docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.8` item 7.**
   - **(iii) THE RESERVED `_value`** — **NOT a fourth kind of member: it is a LITERAL MEMBER whose key is the reserved constant `RESERVED_VALUE_MEMBER` (`docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.1`), distinguished from (i) BY ITS KEY ALONE, and it may stand only where it stands ALONE (`R17`(c); `docs/specs/store-rederive/store-model.md §2.3` item 5).** **A node carrying it is TRANSPARENT ON READ (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.8` item 7); a node carrying it beside anything else is REFUSED at the write (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.5` item 7's arm 9), so the STATE never holds one.** `(A C11 · A R16 · A R17 · CONTRACT + CENSUS)`

7. **⟶ LIST-SHAPED vs DICT-SHAPED — HOW EACH IS RECOGNISED — APPENDED 2026-10-08 (`R16`(e)'s own example, THE ARCHITECT).** **`R16`(e) prints the round trip *"write [true, 123, "Foo"] --> link with nodes of values true, 123, "Foo" --> read [true, 123, "Foo"]"*, so the two SHAPES the condensation must recover are named here, in `C12`'s own terms — *"The child order/key is read when condensing the graph into an array/dict on save or read."***
   - **A LIST-SHAPED NODE is recognised by its CHILD NODES: each child instance carries a `_value` literal (a transparent node, (iii) above), the node itself carries NO literals, and its `parent` anchors' `order` members are exactly the dense range `0..n-1` (`n ≥ 1`). CONDENSED BY ANCHOR `order`: the array's element `k` is the child node at the `parent` anchor whose `order` is `k`, and the element's VALUE is that child's `_value` literal — so the children are condensed AWAY and the list of literals is what the read answers.**
   - **A DICT-SHAPED NODE is recognised by its LITERAL MEMBERS PLUS ITS ANCHORED MEMBERS: the node's own `literals` are data members and each `parent` anchor is an object-valued member. CONDENSED BY ANCHOR `key`: each member's name is its anchor's `key`, whose value is the child's transcription (recursively), with the node's own `literals` as the object's remaining members (`docs/specs/store-rederive/write-and-read.md §2.8` item 3, `C21`).**
   - **`DD-13` IS UNCHANGED BY THIS AND GOVERNED BY IT: the array rule's *"no literals"* condition is exactly the list-shaped recognition above, and `DD-13`'s canonical-decimal-keys condition is exactly `order`'s dense range; the dict arm is the object arm.** **Terms: `2 = 1 (list-shaped) + 1 (dict-shaped)` ✓ (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.2`); and the anchor-borne `order`/`key` pair is `DD-4`'s `2`, read at BOTH condensation sites (`docs/specs/store-rederive/census.md §4.9`).** `(A R16 · A C12 · A C21 · docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A DD-4/DD-13 · T · CONTRACT + CENSUS)` **⟶ CORRECTED BESIDE 2026-10-08 (THE SPEC WRITER, ON THE SUPERVISOR'S REVIEW OF `docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-13`; `RCA-8(d)` ANNOTATE-BESIDE — the as-filed sentence above stands byte-for-byte and THIS clause is the operative statement, POINTING AT THE RE-GRAINED `DD-13` IN ITS OWN CELLS).** **THE AS-FILED SENTENCE IS WRONG IN TWO WAYS, AND BOTH ARE CORRECTED HERE.** **(i) THE CLAIM THAT THE ARRAY RULE'S *"no literals"* CONDITION *"IS EXACTLY the list-shaped recognition above"* IS NOT TRUE AS FILED: list-shaped recognition is NO LITERALS **AND** `_value`-CARRYING CHILD NODES **AND** DENSE `order`s — this item's first bullet, THREE conditions — so *"no literals"* is ONE of the three and NOT the recognition; a node with no literals whose children are ordinary (non-transparent) nodes is NOT list-shaped and condenses to an OBJECT.** **(ii) *"`DD-13` IS UNCHANGED BY THIS AND GOVERNED BY IT"* IS WRONG AS FILED, and the sentence says NOTHING about the TRANSPARENT case: `DD-13` IS RE-GRAINED BY THIS ITEM — the array test is keyed on the CHILDREN, and the canonical-decimal KEY SPELLING is a CONSEQUENCE of the mint rather than the test — and `DD-13` now carries a THIRD ARM, a TRANSPARENT node condensing to ITS LITERAL at BOTH sites, which neither this item nor the as-filed `DD-13` enumerated.** **THE LIVE FORM IS `docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-13`'s re-grained cell read together with this item's three conditions: `DD-13`'s dict arm remains the object arm, its re-grained array arm IS this item's list-shaped recognition, and its value arm is the transparent node's.** **Terms, re-printed WITH their terms: the SHAPES this item names stay `2 = 1 (list-shaped) + 1 (dict-shaped)` ✓ AS FILED, while the CONDENSATION'S ARMS are now `3 = 1 (array) + 1 (object) + 1 (value)` ✓ (`docs/specs/store-rederive/contract.md §0A` `DD-13`;
