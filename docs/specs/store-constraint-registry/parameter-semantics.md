**THE THREE TYPE ALIASES, ACCOUNTED FOR SO THE COVERAGE CLAIM IS COMPLETE AND NOT MERELY ASSERTED (a declared type without a row would be a gate-2 finding).** **`ConstraintName` is `string` and its semantics ARE `PAR-15`'s row** (the caller-supplied name: unit, referent, domain, OUTSIDE arms) — **a row for it would duplicate `PAR-15` and mint a second authority for one parameter.** **`ConstraintRegister` is `ReadonlyArray<ConstraintEntry>`, ordered `(address, position)` ascending — its semantics are `PAR-18`'s report member and its members' rows `PAR-21`…`PAR-25`, and its ORDER is carried at `docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/registers.md §2.5` item 3.** **`CommitRegister` is `ReadonlyArray<PendingEntry>`, ordered by `recordedOpId`'s emission ordinal — its semantics are `PAR-18`'s report member and `PAR-30`, with its members' rows `PAR-26`…`PAR-33`.** **Terms: `3` aliases, `3` accounted (`2` by a row that governs them, `1` by `PAR-15`), `0` unaccounted ✓.** `(T · CONTRACT + CENSUS)`

**THE CITED-AND-NOT-RE-AUTHORED SET, NAMED SO THE COVERAGE CLAIM IS CHECKABLE.** **`4` landed rows carry parameters this unit's clauses mention: `PAR-1` (the address, cited by `docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/registers.md §2.1.1` item 2 and by `PAR-22`/`PAR-31`) · `PAR-7` (the registration record, whose members `constraint`/`repair` `ConstrainRequest` carries unchanged) · `PAR-8` (the two callables, cited by `docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/lifecycle.md §2.3` item 6's arm 4 and by `PAR-24`/`PAR-25`) · `PAR-13` (the closed five-member refusal, cited by `docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/lifecycle.md §2.3` item 6 and by `PAR-15`).** **NO row of that set is re-numbered, re-grained or displaced, and this table is read BESIDE it, never in place of it** (`A3 docs/specs/store-rederive/parameter-semantics.md §6`; `REGISTER-ENTRY-COUNT-IS-NOT-CAPPED-AND-ARCHIVE-IS-THE-TRUTH-MECHANISM`). **Terms: `4 = 4` cited, `0` moved ✓.**
