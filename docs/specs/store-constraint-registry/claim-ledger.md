## Part — the claim ledger: every pinnable claim of this contract with its ONE resolving authority (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3`)

**Contract:** `docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/contract.md` — this part is its **part-map entry `claim-ledger`**, of **KIND `carrier`** (`docs/specs/spec-scope-and-structure/contract.md §C.6` · `§C.11.6`; the map row's KIND cell).

**WHAT THIS PART IS.** The claim ledger is a **rule over text that is already in the spec** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.1`): **every pinnable claim of a contract carries (i) ONE resolving authority citation, or (ii) an explicitly-dispositioned inferred form** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.3` · `§3.4`). **No row is added per claim** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.1`), **and the check is a presence-and-resolution check, never a census** (`§3.1`: *"Nothing in this spec counts requirements; nothing caps them"*). **The rows below are therefore CLAIM GROUPS** — one row per clause family that carries pinnable text — **and the row's citation cell is the ONE authority form the whole family resolves to**, each authority spelled in one of the enum's forms (`§3.3`: **`A1`** a recorded architect statement, `tracker:line` · **`A2`** an ACTIVE `docs/decisions.md` `D-id` · **`A3`** a filed spec `§` · **`A4`** a dossier row `unit-adoption-dossier.md · <identifier>`), or the **`§3.4`** status form where the claim is inferred. **A citation in a sixth spelling is a gate-2 finding** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.3` rule 9).

**THE PINNABLE CLASSES ARE CLOSED AT SIX** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.2`): requirement/prohibition · declared parameter semantics · state or fail-state declaration · numeric or census claim · cross-reference · ordering claim. **Outside the set — and therefore carrying no row — are format/presentation conventions, restatements of a landed rule already cited elsewhere in this contract, pointers to another section of this same contract, and headings** (`§3.2`'s *"Outside the set"* paragraph).

### §L.1 THE INFERRED FORM — **`0` `INFERRED` rows, stated rather than left silent**

**An inferred form is legal ONLY as an explicitly dispositioned status carrying a recorded reason AND a named evaluator, and an `unconfirmed` form does not survive this gate** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.4`; the anti-laundering rule at `§3.3.5` items 1–3). **THIS CONTRACT CARRIES NO `INFERRED` ROW: every clause above resolves to one of the enum's four citation forms at its own text, and no clause of this contract rests on an inference.** **Where a reading is this contract's own and no authority rules it, the clause says so in its own words — as `DECLARED-DEFAULT` with a **priced alternative** and a **reversal clause** — and it is filed under a claim family whose authority is one of the four forms, never as an inference standing in for authority.** **The three declared defaults this contract takes, each with its alternative priced and its reversal named: (i) the `name`-absent default spelling (`docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/registers.md §2.2` item 3, reversed in item 4) · (ii) `pendingBound = 64` as a declared constant rather than a construction input (`docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/lifecycle.md §2.3` item 4, with its reversal) · (iii) the report's carriage as a record pair on the `runWrite` edge (`docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/registers.md §2.5` item 2, with its priced alternative and reversal at item 5).** **Terms: `0` inferred rows, `3` declared defaults, `3` priced alternatives, `3` named reversals ✓.** `(A3 docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.4 · T · CONTRACT)`

### §L.2 THE SUPERSESSION POINTER EDGE — **`0` cross-document edges, `1` intra-document edge, both stated**

**A cross-document supersession edge names path + `§n` (`SUPERSEDES §n` / `SUPERSEDED BY §n`, both directions resolving; a bare `§n` across documents is NOT an edge)** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.3` rule 8; `A2 docs/decisions.md · D-GP-SAD-4`). **THIS CONTRACT SUPERSEDES NO CLAUSE OF ANY FILED SPEC: it ADDS a register record, an argument member, a report and a bound beside the landed store, and every landed clause it touches is CITED AND LEFT STANDING.** **`0` cross-document supersession edges are filed, and the absence is a claim a reader can check: no clause of this contract re-grains, replaces or withdraws a sentence of `docs/specs/store-rederive/**`, and none of that directory's files is edited by this pass.** **Terms: `0` cross-document edges, `2` directions of a non-edge, `0` bare-`§n` cross-document references ✓** — **every cross-document pointer in this contract is written in the `A3` path form (`docs/specs/<unit>/<part>.md §<n>`, with the literal `contract` for a contract document).**

**THE ONE EDGE THIS FILING DOES CARRY IS *INTRA*-DOCUMENT, AND IT IS NAMED SO IT IS NOT MISTAKEN FOR A CROSS-DOCUMENT SUPERSESSION** (`docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.3` rule 8: *"A cross-document supersession edge names path + `§n` … a bare `§n` across documents is not an edge"* — the rule's scope is **cross**-document, and an intra-document re-point is a **pointer**, not an edge): **`A docs/specs/store-constraint-registry-review.md §5(a)` (the register's home, naming the monolith path `docs/specs/store-constraint-registry.md` and recording it as *"declared, and NOT created by this pass"*) is SUPERSEDED BY `A docs/specs/store-constraint-registry-review.md §2` (the same record's spec-gate handoff: *"THE CONTRACT'S HOME IS A DIRECTORY: `docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/contract.md` plus its parts, authored in ONE pass with the index region declaring the map"*).** **Both directions resolve at their own paths, and BOTH are clauses of ONE document — so no path-qualified cross-document edge is owed, and this contract records the re-point rather than letting the two clauses stand unreconciled** (`docs/specs/store-constraint-registry/contract.md §0.2` item 9). **Terms: `1` intra-document edge, `2` sites (`§5(a)` → `§2`), `0` cross-document edges ✓.** `(A docs/specs/store-constraint-registry-review.md §5(a) · A docs/specs/store-constraint-registry-review.md §2 · A3 docs/specs/spec-authoring-discipline/contract.md §3.3 · T · CONTRACT + CENSUS)`
