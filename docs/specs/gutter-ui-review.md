## 1. Step 1 — VALIDITY: **`VALID-WITH-CONDITIONS`**

**IN SCOPE AS A UI UNIT OF AN ADOPTED FAMILY — and the admission is what makes it so.** `E10` is the **UI half** of the `A-d4` panes/zones family, **admitted by the architect's ruling recorded at `docs/pending.md` §I-septies** (*"Park checks until the UI exists, then start UI spec"*; the `PROPOSED — awaiting admission` marking is superseded). **What the validity step found was NOT an admission defect but a SHAPE defect, and the decisive one is recorded below.**

1. **THE DECISIVE FINDING — NO COMPOSITION EXISTED IN THE APP.** The filed contract's `§5.1` allow-list admitted only the affordance module and one authored card in `src/shared/demo-envelope.ts`, while `§2.2`'s prohibitions forbade any other import and all element lookup and `§5.2` itself stated the renderer bundle did not import the module — **so hover, press, drag, release and drop never occurred in any running app, and the unit chartered as *"the place the family becomes observable"* was OBSERVABILITY-EMPTY BY CONSTRUCTION** (`docs/pending.md` §I-octies's `[DECISIVE]` finding). **Its `§5.U` `U-3`–`U-6` rows ("MEASURED at the live gate") were unreachable.**

3. **THE CAPTURE DECISION HAD NO LANDING SITE.** The contract required `capture: true` in the hooks object handed to `controller.attach(element, hooks)`, while `E3`'s frozen `attach(element, hooks?)` builds exactly the four hooks and forwards no `capture` (its sealed clause says the opposite twice) — **so either a new gate on a `DONE`-adjacent module was owed, or the capture claim was vacuous.**
